- **State of play**: server-side complete. The crafting campaign (CR-01 to CR-17, 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-27) built the activity layer on the Phase 1 state:
  - **Requests.** The cell parses every argument of methods 95-100 and forwards a `CraftRequest`. The base answers every verb, and every refusal is a visible text line (D-CR14); a malformed request can only be forged and is dropped with a WARN.
  - **Login.** One reliable bundle after `onClientReady` restores disciplines and expertise (136), all five paradigm levels (138), known blueprints (139), the ASP total and the crafting options (140). Every character starts at Common 5 and the other paradigms at 1 (D-CR03).
  - **Learning and ASP.** `spendAppliedSciencePoints` learns a discipline in one locked, replay-safe transaction that checks the catalog, known, ASP, the paradigm level and prerequisites at expertise 50. A character has 1 ASP at level 1 and earns 1 per level, written with the level (D-CR01).
  - **Stations and tools.** A request needs a crafting station within 5 units (templates with `ENTITYFLAG_Craft_*`, tracked by the cell), a Field Crafting Tool in the crafting bag covering the discipline (D-CR21; never alloying), or the GM's "craft anywhere". The base sends 140 at login and on every change.
  - **The four verbs.** Craft, research, reverse engineering and alloying are validated at the request, queued on a per-player induction engine (one running, ten in all, the type-16 bar with an absolute expiry), and applied when the bar ends in one transaction that re-checks the named items and knowledge, consumes, grants, adds expertise, and rolls back with a line and a resync on any failure. A logout, disconnect or gate travel drops the queue with nothing consumed (D-CR12). Research rolls `100 − expertise + 5 × kickers` and teaches the blueprint that makes the item; reverse-engineering recovery rises with expertise (D-CR06); alloying follows the client's count rules (Normal 10, Good 5, Great 2, Fantastic 1).
  - **Respec.** A player's `.respeccraft` opens a free respec with `onCraftingRespecPrompt(0)` for 60 s; the prompt's Yes clears every discipline and its expertise, refunds the ASP spent learning them, keeps blueprints and paradigm levels, and drops the induction queue (D-CR02, D-CR23; CR-10).
  - **Items.** Using one of the 193 mapped Blueprint items teaches its blueprint; a Racial Paradigm Guide raises its paradigm by 1, to at most 10 (D-CR04, D-CR03, D-CR26). Every grant path puts the `{17,15}` crafting components, guides and Blueprint items in the crafting bag (CR-16), and that bag trades (CR-17) and mails (#933) (D-CR28).
  - **Content and GM tooling.** The stasis-room debug hub has four crafting stations and a crafting-supplies vendor at 1 naquadah per item ([debug-hub.md](../content/debug-hub.md#crafting-corner)); its loot crate drops a Cell, the guides and a Blueprint item. GM `.allcraft`, `.craftkit <blueprint> [count]` and `.learnblueprint <id>` (D-CR17).
  - Nothing has been run in a client yet, so every row is NT until the owner's [CR-14 UAT](../analysis/crafting/handoffs/session-resume.md#cr-14-owner-uat-checklist).
- **Path forward**: the owner's CR-14 UAT; then the carried gaps in the [session resume](../analysis/crafting/handoffs/session-resume.md#known-gaps-carried-forward): re-check the station or tool gate when a job completes, the vendor's `max_stack_size`, crafting stations in playable worlds (a content decision, D-CR05), and loot placement of Blueprint items and guides beyond the debug crate.
