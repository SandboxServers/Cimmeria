## Design decisions

1. **Chain allocation.** 702 → 1261-1265, 703 → 1271-1273 (both inside CA06's 1261-1290), 704 → 1291-1301 (inside CA07's 1291-1320). Gaps left deliberately between the two CA06 missions so a later 702-only or 703-only addition does not interleave.

2. **Dialog 4866 is click-to-play on a new `interact_tag` chain 1299, not played on arrival — a RECONSTRUCTION choice, coordinator-decided.** `executor::dialog::display` (`executor/dialog.rs:47-105`) needs an NPC to bind the client's portrait lookup to, and resolves in order: the chain's `target_entity_id` param, which only `interact_tag` / `interact_template` triggers stamp; the player's `last_interaction_target` pin; a monologue fallback for dialogs whose every screen carries `speaker_id = 0` (`spawner/dialogs.rs:82-103` computes that set); otherwise `warn!` and return. Dialog 4866 carries `speaker_id = 1113` on screens 96892 and 96894 (`dialog_screens.sql:20516,20520`), so it is not a monologue, and chain 1291's region trigger stamps no id of its own.

   Sequence of decisions, recorded because the reasoning moved: the `mission-systems-advisor` pass flagged the briefed `display_dialog 4866` on chain 1291 as a dead action, and I moved it to 1299. The coordinator corrected the diagnosis — the empty pin is a bug in `cell_methods/player/interaction/interact.rs` that the mission-701 packet is fixing — and asked for it back on 1291; I restored it and kept 1299 as well, flagging that even with the pin restored it resolves to the *cell* Zuritska rather than the workstation one, and is empty after a relog. The coordinator's final decision: **1299 only**, because keeping both plays the line twice on a clean run and 1299 already covers the relog case. Chain 1291 keeps the advance, the follow clear and the `!` on `Castle_Zuritska_Comms`, which is what makes the click reachable; chain 1297 restores that bit on relog and 1300 repairs it on region re-entry. Dialog 2580 on the victory chain needs none of this: single screen, `speaker_id = 0` (`dialog_screens.sql:11779`), so the monologue fallback carries it.

   The engine change that would allow an arrival play is a `target_tag` param on `display_dialog` resolving the speaker by tag; the coordinator has recorded it as a campaign follow-up candidate.

3. **Region re-entry repair chains (1265, 1300, 1301).** Because `set_interaction_type` is global on the entity, another player finishing the same step clears the bit for everyone, including a player still on it — and the only repair originally authored was the `player_loaded` restore, i.e. a relog. These three chains re-assert the same bits on `enter_region` with the same step gates, so walking out and back in is enough. `|` is idempotent, so a spurious re-fire costs nothing, and each is disjoint from the advance chain that shares its region trigger (pinned by `chains_1261_and_1265_never_claim_the_same_region_entry` and `the_comms_room_chains_never_claim_the_same_entry`).

4. **A second Romney death chain (1273), not in the packet text as written.** Castle is an open persistent world and region entry is client-hinted, so nothing forces a player through `Castle.InterrogationBlock` before reaching Romney. Chain 1272 alone would leave such a player stuck on step 2403 behind a corpse that only returns on the spawner timer. 1273 advances 2403 → 2404, grants the badge and completes in one action list. The two can never both fire for one kill: `fire_entity_death` (`event_dispatch/lifecycle.rs:73-90`) builds the killer's context **once** and `resolve_event` evaluates every chain against that one snapshot, so `2403 active` and `2404 active` are mutually exclusive at resolve time. Pinned by `only_one_death_chain_claims_a_kill_on_the_expected_path` / `..._on_the_off_path`.

5. **The escort is cleared, not walked home.** The packet asked for `move_waypoint Castle_Zuritska_Cell` to her spawn when step 2405 advances. That needs the spawnlist coordinate packet CA05 authors, which does not exist on this branch, and `parse_destination` defaults a missing coordinate to `(0,0,0)` — dropping her through the floor. `set_npc_ai_state Idle` was considered and rejected: the clear already sets `AiState::Idle`, so it would add nothing. Chain 1291 therefore issues `set_follow_target Castle_Zuritska_Cell {}` (the clear shape) and she stands where the escort ended. **Integration action for the coordinator:** once CA05 publishes the `Castle_Zuritska_Cell` spawn position, add a fourth action to chain 1291.

6. **Objective 4653 is not hand-completed.** `complete_mission 702` completes it, because 4653 is the only objective on 702's last step. Both a `complete_objective` and the mission completion would double-complete. Pinned by a negative assertion in `chain_1263_completes_702_accepts_704_and_starts_the_escort`.

7. **The Livewire single-grant guard lives on the launcher.** Victory chains evaluate no conditions, so the only thing stopping a second Data Crystal is chain 1292's `step_status 704 2406 eq active` plus 1293's own advance to 2407. `chain_1293_is_triggerless_and_conditionless` pins the inversion directly (a condition row on 1293 would read as a guard while guarding nothing), and `chain_1292_does_not_resolve_on_the_delivery_step` pins the gate.

8. **`remove_item` runs before the state moves in chain 1295.** The crystal is handed to Zuritska; consuming first keeps a mid-list failure from leaving a completed mission plus a stale mission item. Per D-CA08 the chain does not gate on holding 5029.

9. **Every `accept_mission` chain carries the `not_active` gate.** 1263 gates on `mission_status 704 eq not_active`, 1295 on `mission_status 706 eq not_active`. On 1263 the gate does double duty: it also prevents a replayed dialog choice from re-arming the escort on a player who already reached the Communications Room.

10. **Restore chains.** Every `set_interaction_type` set has a matched clear and a `player_loaded Castle` restore keyed to the step that needs it: 1264 (702/2419), 1297 (704/2406, both bits), 1298 (704/2407). 1296 re-arms the 704/2405 escort follow — the disconnect destroys the player entity and releases its id for reuse (`space_manager/entities.rs:110-159,308`), and `npc_ai/follow.rs:50-58` clears a vanished target within one AI tick, so re-issuing `use_player` on load is required rather than merely nice. 703 sets no bits and needs no restore.

11. **Test file layout.** Five modules rather than three. `mission_704.rs` reached 1022 lines, over the 700-line hard cap, so it split along two natural seams: `mission_704_restores.rs` takes the relog restores and re-entry repairs (a distinct lifecycle concern, and the cross-step negatives belong with them), and `castle_702_704_executor.rs` takes every test that pushes actions through `execute_actions` or `fire_chain_by_id`. The executor module is organised by risk rather than by mission, following the precedent `chain_replay_tests/mod.rs` already documents for `sgc_w1_move_entity.rs` and `grant_xp.rs`; it also removes a duplicated Castle `SpaceManager` fixture. Final sizes, all under the hard cap: `mission_702.rs` 565, `mission_703.rs` 467, `mission_704.rs` 682, `mission_704_restores.rs` 329, `castle_702_704_executor.rs` 375. The seed SQL is 747 lines and is data, which the ledger's architecture guardrails exempt until 1500.

12. **Assertion ordering in the tests.** Several invariant checks (no `complete_objective`, no `display_dialog` on a region chain, exactly one grant) were originally written *after* the action-label signature assertion, which maps any unexpected action to `"OTHER"` and therefore always trips first. They documented an invariant they could not enforce. All of them now run before the signature check. Flagged by the `mission-systems-advisor` pass.

## Known gaps and flags for the coordinator

1. **HIGH — the escort actor is shared too.** There is one `Castle_Zuritska_Cell` entity in the zone and `follow_target_id` is a single field on it, so if player B frees Zuritska while player A is mid-escort she re-points at B and A's escort visually ends. Mission state is unaffected: A's step 2405 still advances on A's own `Castle.CommsRoom` entry. A per-player escort would need owner-scoped entities, which is design gate GCA1, not a content packet. Recorded in the seed header; no fix attempted.

2. **HIGH — `set_interaction_type` is zone-wide in a shared world (D-CA15 tension).** The arm mutates `CellEntity::interaction_type_flags` and fans the new value to every witness (`executor/world/mod.rs:19-65`); `docs/content/interaction-flags.md:190` states this explicitly. In Castle that means player A entering the Interrogation Block lights the `!` over Zuritska's cell actor for **everyone**, and player A freeing her **clears** it for a player B who is mid-step 2419 — B cannot click the actor again until B's next `player_loaded` fires the restore chain, i.e. until B relogs. The step conditions mean nothing *fires* for the wrong player, so this is an indicator/interactivity defect, not mission-state corruption. It is nonetheless a real shared-world bug and it applies equally to the Comms terminal and the workstation Zuritska. The per-player fix is packet CA02's widened `add_dialog_set` bind (D-CA03); once that lands, every `set_interaction_type` pair in this file should be re-authored onto dialog-set binds. Until then it is the only primitive that makes these actors clickable, and the `interact_tag` linter requires one of the two. Documented in the seed header and in `mission-chains.md`.

3. **BLOCKING FOR THE LINTER — region point sets do not exist yet.** `crates/content-engine/tests/interact_tag_linter.rs::every_chain_region_key_matches_a_seeded_point_set` requires every `enter_region` trigger key to byte-match a `point_sets.name` row. `Castle.InterrogationBlock` and `Castle.CommsRoom` are packet CA05's contract and are not in `db/resources/Events/Seed/point_sets.sql` on this branch, so that test fails on exactly those two names, once per chain that triggers on them (1261, 1265, 1271 on the first; 1291, 1300, 1301 on the second). See "Commands run" for the verification that it passes once the two rows exist. The other linter test, `every_interact_tag_chain_has_set_interaction_type`, passes with no allowlist entry needed — all three `interact_tag` tags (`Castle_Zuritska_Cell`, `Castle_CommsTerminal`, `Castle_Zuritska_Comms`) have a `set_interaction_type` action in the same file.

4. **Actor tags do not exist yet, and three column values on them are load-bearing.** `Castle_Zuritska_Cell`, `Castle_Zuritska_Comms`, `Castle_Romney`, `Castle_CommsTerminal` are CA05 contracts; `find_entity_by_tag` misses are a `debug!` and a no-op, so the chains are inert but harmless until CA05 lands, and the chain-replay tests do not need them. Three per-row values CA05 must set explicitly, each verified against current engine code during the `npc-ai-spawn-advisor` review and each a mission-breaker at the column default:
   - **`spawnlist.respawn_secs` on `Castle_Romney`** (DB `CHECK >= 3`). **No row in the shipped `spawnlist.sql` sets it and no `entity_templates` row does either**, so `COALESCE(s.respawn_secs, t.respawn_secs)` (`cell/spawner/npcs.rs:149`) is NULL for every NPC in the game, `normalize_respawn_secs(None)` returns `None`, and `mark_npc_dead` skips the `respawn_at` stamp (`cell/combat/state.rs:110`). Nothing in Cimmeria respawns today. Without an explicit value Romney is one-shot and mission 703 is uncompletable for every player after the first.
   - **`entity_templates.move_speed` on Zuritska's template.** The COALESCE default is 0.6 units/tick = 6.0 u/s against a player run speed of 8.125 u/s, and `npc_ai/follow.rs:80-84` only re-paths when `nav_path` is empty, so at the default she diverges monotonically and never re-enters the 2-5 unit follow band. The only non-default row in the seed is template 10 "Col Marsh (pet)" at 0.9.
   - **`wander_radius` 0/NULL and `patrol_path_id` NULL on Zuritska's template**, or `npc_ai/dispatch.rs:107-117` promotes her to Patrol/Wander and she walks off both before the escort starts and again the tick after chain 1291 clears the follow.

5. **HIGH — one point of splash damage ends the escort until the player relogs.** Follow is preemptable into Fighting by any threat (`combat/threat/aggro.rs:66-75`) and `npc_ai_leash` ends at `AiState::Idle`, never back to Follow (`npc_ai/leash.rs:57`). GC1b-0 stops Zuritska being teleported to spawn but not from stopping. The only re-fire of `set_follow_target` authored here is chain 1296 on `player_loaded`. The cheap repair, deliberately **not** taken because it changes the indicator lifecycle the packet specified: move chain 1263's `!` clear into chain 1291 so the cell actor stays clickable through step 2405, and add an `interact_tag Castle_Zuritska_Cell` + `step_status 704 2405 eq active` chain that re-arms the follow on a click. That is a design call for the coordinator, not a seed detail.

6. **A damage-over-time killing blow fires no `entity_death` at all.** `cell/effects/pulsing/tick.rs::fire_pulse` mutates the health stat with no alive→dead detection, and `mark_npc_dead` is only reachable from `abilities/damage_apply/mod.rs` and `abilities/death.rs`. A Romney finished by a bleed tick sits at 0 HP with neither 1272 nor 1273 firing — and, per gap 4 above, never resets. This is an engine gap rather than this packet's, but 703's only completion path is an `entity_dead_tag` chain, so it is recorded in the seed's engine-facts block.

7. **Region entry is not re-evaluated at login.** `enter_region` is raised only from the client method `triggerClientHintedGenericRegion` (`cell/cell_methods/player/world/mod.rs:80-89`). A player who logs out standing inside `Castle.InterrogationBlock` on step 2402, or inside `Castle.CommsRoom` on 2405, logs back in with no advance and has to walk out and back in. 703 has chain 1273 as its net; 702 and 704 have none, and there is no `InRegion` condition to gate a `player_loaded` advance on. Documented rather than worked around, because the alternative (a redundant `interact_tag` advance chain on each step) adds a second completion path to two steps that already have one.

8. **Straight-line follow is provisional.** Castle has no `castle.nav`, so `find_path` returns `None` (`space_manager/spatial.rs:49`) and `npc_ai/follow.rs:110` pushes the destination as a single waypoint. Expect Zuritska to clip through Castle interior geometry on the 704 escort until packet CA14 produces the navmesh. Recorded, not fixed.

9. **Livewire for the Communications Terminal is provisional (D-CA09).** The original minigame id for step 2406 is unrecovered.

10. **Dialogs 2577 and 2581 have no `dialog_screen_buttons` rows.** Chains 1263 and 1295 trigger on `dialog_choice`, which the client sends as `dialogButtonChoice`. The existing Cellblock precedent already relies on this shape — chain 1018 triggers on `dialog_choice 5020`, and dialog 5020 likewise has zero button rows — so the client evidently sends a choice for the terminal screen of a buttonless dialog. Not verified against a live client in this packet; it is a UAT item for milestones M2 and M3.

11. **No `grant_xp`.** Missions 701-708 all carry `reward_xp 0` and there are no `mission_reward_groups` rows, matching the ledger's "Mission XP" non-goal (GC3).

12. **Out of scope, confirmed:** Romney's Files (item 2698) has no acquisition evidence anywhere and is excluded per CA06's own scope line; the legacy Romney laundry-crate flow (dialogs 522-524) stays disabled.
