> **Type:** reference. **Audience:** the orchestrator, the brief writers (SP-01 to SP-05), the lab drivers and verifiers.
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-02 (SP-L1: drive-packet column and totals synced to the briefs on disk)
> **Companions:** [README](README.md) (ledger and decisions), [work-packets.md](work-packets.md) (the SP-00 spec and the UAT contract), [RESUME.md](RESUME.md), [unified-uat.md](../../guides/unified-uat.md).

This is the single list of every client row the four-zone chain still owes, with the drive packet that grades it. The **Grade** column is empty: it is filled by the results PR of each lab wave (PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NEEDS_HUMAN, per the contract in work-packets.md). Source links point at the ledger that owns the row; when two ledgers describe one row, both are named and the first is the primary.

## How to read the table

- **Row id** is the id the source uses. Where two sources reuse an id, the id is qualified: unqualified `S1` to `S8` are Castle mission 1521; `hub S1` to `hub S5` are the hub quartermaster. `A1` to `A5` are Castle CS-00B; they are not the Harset letter steps `A1.x` in the Harset guide. `T18` in the Tollana rows is the 568 double-click row; the Cellblock class-crate rows T18/T19 are graded as CB-U07. A source id that looks like another campaign's packet id (`CS-03d-U1` to `CS-03d-U5`) is code-formatted in the first cell, so the ledger prefix check (`tools/agent-skills/test_ledgers.py`) does not read it as an SP row declaring prefix CS.
- **Drive packet** is the brief part that grades the row, taken from the `ROWS:` line of each brief on disk (SP-L1 sync). A packet over the 22-call cap is written as parts `<packet>-p<n>` (contract § C2a), so a row maps to one or more parts (`SP-DCB1-p6, p7, p8, p9`), and a row split across parts carries a letter in the brief (`CB-U03a` to `CB-U03d` grade `CB-U03`). Packets that SP-00 added are under [Split changes](#split-changes). A cell reading `(no brief)` marks a row with no brief or verify file yet.
- **Fixture or setup** names the start state. Every fixture is traced from the cited source; a brief writer re-traces ids from `db/resources/` before relying on it (contract § C7 rule 4).
- The Cellblock **T** rows are the visual halves of the unified-uat T01-T25 rows: the 2026-10-21 colo run confirmed their server side, and only a screenshot of the client can settle the rest.
