## Ruling: option (b), refuse the trade

A trade of a mission-only item (`container_sets = {2}`) is **refused** as untradeable. Keep the new `grant_container` rule as it is. Don't add a separate placement function for trade, and don't add 2 to `TRADEABLE_CONTAINERS`.

Why:

- **Mission items are not tradeable by design.** `TRADEABLE_CONTAINERS` leaves out the mission bag (2). `TradeAbort::IneligibleContainer`'s doc names the "mission-item-share" exploit as one it closes. Items carry no mission flag; the mission bag is the only marker (bank-vault worknote bv-03). The seed has 749 `{2}` types. 657 of them lack `CAN_BE_SOLD` (1024), the flag the original Python trade gated on (`deprecated/python/cell/Trade.py:49`, `item.canSell()`). No seeded set holds 2 together with anything else.
- **A `{2}` item in bag 1 is misplaced.** The move path refuses to put a `{2}` item into 1 or 15 (`item_allows_container`, `move_/finish.rs:60`). After P08, every grant sends it to 2. The only ways it reaches bag 1 are the #1675 bug (old rows) and vendor purchases (a follow-up already listed in the README). Under the old rule, a trade copied that misplacement into the recipient's backpack and handed a quest item to a player who may not have the mission.
- **The player sees why, and can fix it.** `plan_destinations` returns `TradeAbort::NoDestination`. `refusal_lines` already maps that to "Trade cancelled: one of the items you offered cannot be traded." for the offerer, and the matching line for the partner. Both sides get `Cancelled`. The mission bag is player-movable (`player_movable(2) == Movable::Yes`) and the item allows 2, so the owner can drag a stranded item into the mission bag.
- **Rejected (a), keep trade on the old rule.** It reopens mission-item sharing through trade and needs a second placement rule that must be kept in step with the grant one.
- **Rejected: route the traded item to the recipient's mission bag.** That means changing the whitelist, the lock set and the slot reservation. It turns mission-item sharing into a feature, and it is a design change outside a bug fix.

This needs no seed change, no wire change and no client patch. The refusal message already exists. **Not a BlockedDecision.**
