Decided from the repo:

- **Faction 1.** Templates 54 and 59 use it. A player cannot attack faction 1, it reads friendly to players, no faction is hostile to it, and a faction-1 NPC with no patrol or wander gets no AI turn. The reaction table also has 18 `Jaffa_Dakara`, which no seeded template uses. The npc-ai-spawn-advisor advised against it: 18 and the hostile faction 10 are neutral to each other, so it would not make the cast fight Ba'al's troops, and its one hostile cell (21 `Jaffa_Ra`) would put every cast NPC on the NPC-target scan.
- **Looks.** Every look is copied whole from another template, so the Debug Area lineup needs no new actor. The three armed Jaffa and Rak'nor share the Standard Jaffa armour Harset's Free Jaffa (206-208) wear. Ba'al copies 167 Sandbox Ba'al, his head in the Praxis Goa'uld armour (dialog 5840 has Moh'katan speak of "Baal and his Praxis alliance"), not 42, which is his head on a bare body.
- **A copy needs a higher id than its source.** The lineup tags each actor with the lowest template id that wears its look. The first draft copied Ba'al from 1401; 443 is lower, so `debug_area_lineup_live_db_nameplates_and_tags_name_the_source` asked for actor 1539 to be renamed after 443. Copying from 167 instead leaves the lineup as it is.
- **`respawn_secs` on props.** Set because the campaign's seed rules ask for it on every template. It has no effect on a `being`: the respawn tick walks mobs only, and a faction-1 prop cannot die.
- **Flap bit.** A flap is for everyone at any time, so `INT_MissionWorldObject` is on the template, as the DHD and the ring switch carry theirs. The aoi-witness-broadcast review confirmed that `add_dialog_set` and `remove_dialog_set` keep a template bit, and that `set_interaction_type` with `"op": "="` replaces it for every witness. **DK-04 must use `|` and `~` on the flaps, never `=`.**

## Open Questions

1. **Two unnamed gate officers, one name string.** Speaker 2959 (dialogs 6106, 6107) talks at the Western Gate; speaker 2958 (dialog 5823) is the "Jaffa Commander at the Eastern tents" of step 4911. The client has one string, `DN_npc_int_JaffaCaptain_DakaraE1`. Template 442 carries 2959, as the packet says. The audit attributes step 4911 to 2959; the step's dialog is 5823, speaker 2958. A template's speaker id does not decide which dialog it can show (the label comes from the entity the dialog is pinned to), so DK-21 can spawn 442 twice or add a second template in 458-459.
2. **Every look is a placeholder.** The four Jaffa share one armour and one head. Client meshes that would fit better but that no template uses yet, so none is proven to draw as a prop: `GP-Props.GP-B_FieldOps_Corpse00` to `04` and `GP-Jaffa_Corpse00` to `04` (remains), `GP-Props.GP-SuperWeapon_Console00` (terminal or power supply), `Em-Props.EM-Naq_Generator00`, `Em-Props.EM-SupplyCrate00` to `02`, `GA-Props.Ga-Curtain00` and `01` (a flap). The owner chooses after seeing M2; `live_db_every_dakara_e1_look_is_one_another_template_wears` then needs the row exempted.
3. **`display_name` or a borrowed client string.** Four of the six empty names have a client string elsewhere with fitting text: 7439 "Loth'ta" (Dakara E2), 8320 "Jaffa Captain" (Yotunheim), 7550 "Terminal", 8545 "Ring Controls" (Dakara E3). Pointing `name_id` at one of those renders the client's own text, as Harset did for three of its names, at the cost of naming a Dakara_E1 actor by another zone's string. The packet asked for `display_name`; switching is one column per row.
4. **`display_name` on a prop is unproven in a client.** The lineup proved it on mobs. The server sends it to a `being` the same way; whether the nameplate draws it is an M2 check on 448 and 453-455.
5. **The flap bit is the quest-object glow.** No other bit fits a door. If the glow reads wrong in the client, the alternative is no bit on the template and one raised by a chain.
6. **"No ability set beyond a default."** Read here as: no new set, and the Jaffa staff set 4 on the three rows whose copied look holds a staff, as the source template 206 has it. If the intended reading is no set at all, set the three to NULL; nothing at runtime changes, because a faction-1 NPC never fights.
7. **Levels are a choice.** 50 on the named four matches templates 54 and 59; 5 on the warrior is the top of the arc's mission levels. Loth'ta's later strings put him at 28 (E2) and 35 (E3); no E1 level exists.
8. **The hologram is a solid Ba'al.** No effect makes it look projected.
9. **"Destroy the power supply."** 456 and 457 are friendly interactables. If DK-32 wants them shot, they need hostile templates in 460-479, as Harset's talk-and-kill pairs do.
10. **Which interaction set belongs to which flap.** Sets 1912-1915 alternate Enter and Exit, the name strings go To, To, From, From. `static_interaction_sets` is left empty on the flaps rather than record a guess; the server does not read it for them.
11. **The escort.** 444's moniker says escort. Its `move_speed` is NULL (0.6 per tick); the seed's follower rows use 0.9 to keep pace with a running player. A packet that makes it follow should set that.
12. **Moh'katan's nameplate.** Template 54 shows string 8259, "Moh'Katan"; every Dakara dialog and string spells it "Moh'katan". Not edited (shared with Harset).
