## The Rust Change

`quiet_cooldown` is a boolean param, `false` when absent, and the loader drops a row that sets it `true` without `cooldown_secs` or gives it a non-boolean value. It travels as `Action::SendSystemMail.quiet_cooldown` and `ContentMailCooldown.quiet`. On the base, one new match arm in `handle_content_system_mail` catches a cooldown refusal of a quiet mail, logs `content.send_system_mail reason=cooldown quiet=true` at DEBUG on target `mail` and returns. Nothing else on the path changed: the claim SQL, the transaction, the sent line, the notify and every other refusal are the code they were.

**Design choices.**

- **Quiet covers the cooldown refusal only.** A quiet mail's first firing still gets its "sent you mail" line, and "the mail service is unavailable" or a writer refusal still gets its line and its WARN. Those are faults, and the claim rolls back with them, so the notice is retried at the next entry.
- **The flag lives on the cooldown, not on the mail.** `ContentMailCooldown.quiet` cannot be set without a window, which is the only state it means anything in.
- **Once per character is `i32::MAX` seconds.** The claim binds the window as `i32`, so 2147483647 is the longest it honours (about 68 years). A test steps the clock ten years and is still refused.
- **DEBUG on `mail`, not WARN on `content`.** The refusal happens at every login of every notified character. It is the expected outcome, not an event. `content` ships at INFO, and the server's target scan refuses a DEBUG row there, so the row uses `mail`, which ships DEBUG.
- **No world condition beside the trigger key.** `player_loaded`'s key is the engine's world filter, and the dispatcher takes the world from its caller. A second gate would have to agree with it.
- **The seed comments call archetype 7 by its enum name.** 7 is `ARCHETYPE_Sholva` (SGU, char_defs 8 and 18), which the ledgers call the Free Jaffa. `ARCHETYPE_Jaffa` is 8, the Praxis Jaffa. The tests use both.

**Proof that existing mail behaviour is unchanged.**

- The two existing seeded uses (chains 7011 and 13007) carry no `quiet_cooldown`. Their replay tests assert the exact `ContentSystemMail`, now including `quiet: false`, and pass.
- The existing base test `live_db_content_mail_sends_once_then_refuses_inside_the_cooldown` still asserts the wait line and the WARN for a non-quiet refusal, unchanged, and passes.
- The new base test runs the same second firing twice, once quiet and once not: the non-quiet one gets the wait line and a WARN.
- Existing literals changed only by gaining `quiet_cooldown: false` or `quiet: false`.

## Trust Boundary

- **Who fires it.** `fire_player_loaded` has one production caller, the cell's `InitPlayerState` handler. The base sends that message from `handle_on_client_ready`, which takes `pending_client_ready` out of the session, so a client that repeats `onClientReady` gets nothing the second time. The world name and the archetype in the message come from the base's own session and the character's database row.
- **Another archetype.** `archetype eq 7` reads the cell entity's archetype, and a missing value reads as -1, so it fails closed. Tested for archetypes 1 to 6, 8 and an unstamped entity, resolved against the whole seed.
- **Another world.** The trigger matches the world name exactly. Tested against every `resources.worlds` row, `Dakara_E1_StoryRm` included.
- **Repeats.** The grant is idempotent in memory and in SQL. The mail is one row-locked transaction per world entry that finds the claim and writes nothing; the cell's one-second per-chain debounce bounds it further.
- **What a client gains by forcing it.** Nothing it would not get anyway: one address the character is entitled to and one mail with no attachment.

## Open Questions

1. **Is the mail window the right place for the notice?** The ledger already lists it as a lab item. The first firing also prints a chat line, so a player who never opens mail still sees that something arrived, but not what it says.
2. **Sender and wording.** "Dakara Gate Watch" and the three sentences are this project's text. They are one seed row.
3. **Should an older Free Jaffa get the notice?** A Free Jaffa created before #1273 who reaches Dakara later gets the grant and the notice on that first entry. Both are true for them too, so the chains do not try to tell the two apart.
4. **The pin's 10 m.** It clears the steps and leaves the DHD 6 m ahead. A client walk may prefer the dais top or the plaza centre; the guard accepts 5 to 15 m on the axis only after `PIN` is updated with it.
