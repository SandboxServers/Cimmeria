## Checks and limits

The cell-content chain replay loads the whole seeded engine, resolves all four flaps with correct, wrong and missing world context, then fires the real dispatcher and asserts one `GateTravel` to the expected world and point. The cell-world live-DB guards pin the four spawn rows, four regions and three respawners, load both shipped navmeshes, and check each point reaches the respective plaza or interior anchor. A missing mesh fails the guard. These tests need a seeded PostgreSQL database; a local `require_db_or_skip!` pass alone does not exercise the assertions.

M2 UAT remains: walk the estimated flaps, enter through each, exit through both, relog inside world 62, and inspect whether the regions and Med Tent are visible at usable positions. Nothing in an automated replay proves the client's rendered flap placement, collision, discovery text or relog view. Corrections should cite `PL-DK-A-*` or `PL-DK-B-*` row ids and update the seed, guard and ledger together.
