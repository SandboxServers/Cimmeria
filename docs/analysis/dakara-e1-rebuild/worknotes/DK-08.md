# DK-08: any-order objective ruling

> Type: reference.

Ruling for the three steps where the player may finish objectives in any order: 1570/4902, 1570/4901 and 1646/4908. Seed-only answer; no engine change. Rows are shaped as C4 (chain id, trigger, conditions, actions). Chain ids follow C3 where they fit; the overflow is in the open questions.

Status after review: 4901 and 4908 are ruled with exact rows. 4902 has its chain pattern but **no trigger**: the evidence names no prop or region for its three objectives (open question 1). DK-10c cannot be filled until that is decided.

## Facts the ruling rests on

- `complete_objective` (`crates/cell-content/src/cell/missions/progression.rs`) calls `mission.complete()` when every non-optional objective of the current step is complete. `mission.complete()` (`crates/entity/src/missions.rs:91`) ends the whole mission. So no chain may complete the last required objective of a step.
- Optional objectives are excluded from that check, so completing an optional objective cannot close a step while a required one is open. `advance_step` completes every still-open objective of the step it leaves, optional and hidden ones included (`progression.rs`, the `old_objectives` loop), so an optional objective with no chain is checked off when the step ends.
- `objective_status` reads `mission_{m}_obj_{o}_status`. The current step's objectives come from `active_objectives`; earlier completed ids come from `completed_objectives` (`crates/cell-content/src/cell/content/mission_context.rs:159-192`). Anything absent evaluates `not_active`, so `neq completed` is true for it.
- Completed objectives survive a relog: the `CompleteObjective` executor arm persists a `MissionUpdate` (`executor/mission.rs:357-425`) and `player_init/mission_restore.rs:42-52,170` restores `completed_objective_ids` as `STATUS_COMPLETED`. The `H41-B1` block in `db/resources/Content/Seed/harset_goauld_chains.sql` (lines 72-89) says otherwise; that comment is stale for objectives. Not edited here.
- Counters are not persisted (`crates/entity/src/cell_entity/entity_struct.rs:795-797`). A world entry builds a new cell entity (`player_init/mod.rs:469-475`: only a same-world respawn keeps it), so counters reset on relog and on every world change, including a tent trip. A missing counter reads 0 (`conditions/mod.rs:360`).
- Chains are resolved before any action runs, so a sibling reads the PRE-increment counter (`executor/counter.rs:12-18`).
- Condition operators: `eq`, `neq`, `lt`, `lte`, `gt`, `gte` (`loader/condition.rs:172-181`). Conditions are AND-only. There is no item-count condition.
- Castle's `messhall_kills` / `hallway05_kills` chains are the counter precedent (`castle_cellblock_chains.sql` ~1715-1780): `increment_counter` with `{"amount": 1}` and the name in `target_key`, a `counter` condition, `reset_counter` on the advancing chain.
- **A chain that handles a click suppresses the bound dialog.** `fire_interact_tag` returns `!resolved.actions.is_empty()`, and `handle_interact` (`cell_methods/player/interaction/interact.rs:183-248`) runs the dsm fallback dialog only when no chain matched. So a prop whose click a chain handles shows nothing unless the chain says `display_dialog`. The dsm keeps the prop clickable; it does not show the dialog.
- **The tag chain fires on every in-range click, bound or not.** Removing the dsm is not a server-side one-shot guard; a repeat click reaches the chain. Every chain below guards itself with conditions.

- Step 4909's turn-in bind (dsm 6744 on Moh'katan, world 62) comes from DK-12b's `player_loaded` restore on `Dakara_E1_StoryRm`.

## Tests (DK-10c for 4902 and 4901; DK-12c for 4908)

Each chain: the fire state and the nearest state that must not fire. Every test asserts a non-empty label list (revert proof). Every trigger that displays asserts `display_dialog_count == 1` (C7).

4902 (DK-10c, after question 1):

- 8060 fires with 5632 active, 5630 active. Must not fire with 5632 already completed (own guard), and must not fire with 5630 completed (8061 owns that state).
- 8061 fires with 5632 active, 5630 completed. Must not fire with 5630 active.

- Relog case: after 5630 completed, reload; 8061 must still be the only one that fires on 5632 (the objective status restored).

4901 (DK-10c):

- 8065/8067/8069 fire with their own marker 0 and `sg18_remains` 0 or 1, and display once. Must not fire with `sg18_remains` 2, nor with their own marker 1.
- 8066/8068/8070 fire with their own marker 0 and `sg18_remains` 2. Must not fire with `sg18_remains` 1, nor with their own marker 1. The click that "re-uses" a remains (marker 1, `sg18_remains` 2) fires nothing: this is the revert proof for the visited marker.
- 8071 fires on `player_loaded` in step 4901 and not in 4905 (step not active).
- Relog case: all counters are 0 after reload; the same three remains re-arm; the step does not advance on the first or second pickup.

4908 (DK-12c):

- Each count chain fires with O active and counter below 4; must not fire with O completed.
- Each last chain fires with O active and counter 4; must not fire with counter 3 or with O completed.
