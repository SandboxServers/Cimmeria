# DK-08: any-order objective ruling

> Type: reference.

Ruling for the three steps where the player may finish objectives in any order: 1570/4902, 1570/4901 and 1646/4908. Seed-only answer; no engine change. Rows are shaped as C4 (chain id, trigger, conditions, actions). Chain ids follow C3.

## Facts the ruling rests on

- `complete_objective` (`crates/cell-content/src/cell/missions/progression.rs`) calls `mission.complete()` when every non-optional objective of the current step is complete. `mission.complete()` (`crates/entity/src/missions.rs:91`) ends the whole mission. So no chain may complete the last required objective of a step.
- Optional objectives are excluded from that check, so completing an optional objective cannot close a step while a required one is open.
- `objective_status` reads `mission_{m}_obj_{o}_status`. The current step's objectives come from `active_objectives`; earlier completed ids come from `completed_objectives` (`crates/cell-content/src/cell/content/mission_context.rs:159-192`). Anything absent evaluates `not_active`, so `neq completed` is true for it.
- Completed objectives survive a relog: `mission_restore.rs` restores `completed_objective_ids` as `STATUS_COMPLETED`, and `missions.rs:122-128` records the H50 persistence. The `H41-B1` block in `db/resources/Content/Seed/harset_goauld_chains.sql` (lines 72-89) says otherwise; that comment is stale for objectives. Not edited here.
- Counters are not persisted (`crates/entity/src/cell_entity/entity_struct.rs:795-797`). They reset on relog and on a new entity.
- Condition operators in use: `eq`, `neq`, `lt`, `lte`, `gt`, `gte` (`crates/content-engine/src/loader/condition.rs:174-179`). Condition types: `mission_status`, `step_status`, `archetype`, `objective_status`, `counter`, `stat_below_max`, `world`, `entity_tag_state`. There is no item-count condition.
- Castle's `messhall_kills` / `hallway05_kills` chains are the counter precedent (`castle_cellblock_chains.sql` ~1715-1780): `increment_counter` with `{"amount": 1}`, a `counter` condition, `reset_counter` on the advancing chain.

## Ruling per step

No archetype split is needed in any of the three steps: dialogs 5814, 6224-6226 and 5821 are speaker-0 only. Every chain below is archetype-neutral.

## Tests (DK-10c for 4902 and 4901; DK-12c for 4908)

Each chain: the fire state and the nearest state that must not fire. Every test asserts a non-empty label list (revert proof).

4902 (DK-10c):

- 8060 fires with 5632 active, 5630 active. Must not fire with 5632 already completed (own guard), and must not fire with 5630 completed (8061 owns that state).
- 8061 fires with 5632 active, 5630 completed. Must not fire with 5630 active.
- 8062/8063 mirror 8060/8061 with the objectives swapped.
- 8064 fires with 5631 active on any state of the pair. Must not fire with 5631 completed.
- Relog case: after 5630 completed, reload; 8061 must still be the only one that fires on 5632 (the objective status restored).

4901 (DK-10c):

- 8065/8067/8069 fire with counter 0 or 1 and dsm present. Must not fire with counter 2.
- 8066/8068/8070 fire with counter 2. Must not fire with counter 1.
- 8071 fires on `player_loaded` in step 4901 and not in 4905 (step not active).
- Relog case: counter is 0 after reload; the same remains re-arm; the step does not advance on the first click.

4908 (DK-12c):

- Each count chain fires with O active and counter below 4; must not fire with O completed.
- Each last chain fires with O active and counter 4; must not fire with counter 3.
- Each restore pair fires after relog with O completed (counter rebuild) and with O active (bind restore); must not double-count.
- Relog case: drops 1 and 2 completed, relog, the counter reads 2 (8155-8156), drops 3 and 4 raise it to 4, and drop 5 advances.
