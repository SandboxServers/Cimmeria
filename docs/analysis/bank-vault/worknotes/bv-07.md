## Evidence

- **`onOrgMoveItemResult` is not a client method.** It is a *cell* method in `SGWInventoryManager.def:211-215` (`UINT8 bSuccess, INT32 orgMoveTag, PYTHON changesToApply`), the legacy base-to-cell callback of an org move, with no Lua subscriber (audit A-13). The brief's "use `onOrgMoveItemResult` where the client expects it" therefore has no client target: refusals use the personal vault's line plus snap-back.
- **`onClearOrgVaultInventory` (74)** has a native handler (`Inventory_HandleOnClearOrgVaultInventory` @ `0x00e1dcc0`) and no Lua subscriber; which container it clears for an org id is not established (`inventory-state-machine.md:264`). Ghidra was not running this session, so it is not used. Stale views are handled server-side instead (see Design).
- **No seeded item lists 19 or 20** in `container_sets` (0 of 6,059; 5,252 list 17), so `item_allows_container(type, 19)` would refuse everything. Entry into an org vault uses the personal vault's rule: the item must allow 17.
- **`sgw_inventory` queries by `item_id` alone exist** (for example `inventory/ammo.rs:228`, `DELETE FROM sgw_inventory WHERE item_id = $1`), which is why org rows are not in that table.

## Design

### Storage (with `database-persistence`, read-only consult)

- **Chosen: a standalone `sgw_organization_vault_items`**, the `sgw_gate_mail_item` precedent: every instance column, `PRIMARY KEY (item_id)`, `UNIQUE (org_id, container_id, slot_id) DEFERRABLE INITIALLY IMMEDIATE` (a two-slot swap is one `UPDATE`), `org_type` pinned by a composite key to `(org_id, org_type)` with `CHECK ((org_type = 1 AND container_id = 19) OR (org_type = 2 AND container_id = 20))`, `slot_id` 0-99, `item_id >= 10000`, `stack_size > 0`, `CHECK (NOT bound)`, and `ON DELETE RESTRICT` to the organization (D-BV18).
- **Rejected: `sgw_inventory.character_id` nullable plus `org_id`** (`num_nonnulls(...) = 1`, a partial unique index). One `UPDATE` per move, but every existing `sgw_inventory` writer could then reach org rows, several by `item_id` alone.
- **Rejected: `INHERITS (sgw_inventory_base)`**: rows would show in every `SELECT` on the base table (the mail table's reason).
- **`item_id` uniqueness across tables** rests on the one sequence plus delete-and-insert moves, as for mail escrow; the consult judged a registry table or trigger not worth it. The live-DB tests assert no id is in two of `sgw_inventory`, the vault and mail escrow after every move shape.
- **`sgw_organization_vault_log`**: one row per committed move, in the move transaction, with `account_id`, `player_id`, `org_id`, `org_type`, `rank`, `direction`, `kind`, the item, the quantity, both ends and their stacks before and after, `tx_id`. No foreign keys (it must outlive a disband and a character delete; a RESTRICT key would block the empty-vault disband). Cash columns are left to BV-08.
- **`sgw_organizations.vault_slots smallint DEFAULT 40 CHECK (40..100, % 10 = 0)`**: the Team vault's size; a Command's is 100 in Rust and ignores the column. BV-09 grows it (`vault_slots = vault_slots + 10 WHERE vault_slots < 100`, one row).
- **The predicate**: `org_vault_is_empty_sql` is `NOT EXISTS (vault row) AND COALESCE(cash = 0, true)`; the Rust `api::org_vault_is_empty` runs `SELECT org_vault_is_empty_sql($1)`, so the two cannot disagree. `VAULT_EMPTY_OVERRIDE` is kept for ORG-06's tests.

### Lock order

- The consult found a real cycle in the obvious order: a withdrawal's `sgw_inventory` insert takes `KEY SHARE` on the mover's `sgw_player` row through its foreign key, after `lock_org`, while a character delete holds that row and waits on its organizations. So every vault action takes **`FOR KEY SHARE` on the actor's `sgw_player` row before `lock_org`** (`org_vault/access.rs::lock_actor`).
- The server-authority review found the second: vendor purchases take `(player, 0)` then `sgw_player FOR UPDATE`, and trades `(player, 1)` then `FOR UPDATE`, so `KEY SHARE` held while waiting on the advisory locks deadlocks against them. A move now takes **the per-player advisory locks first** (`move_/org/rows.rs::move_locks`: `(player, 0)`, then the carried container's), then `KEY SHARE`, then `lock_org` and the membership read, then the rows. The carried container is read unlocked (the move lock already excludes the player's other moves) and re-checked after the row lock. `organization/api.rs` § "Lock order" records both.

### The open (BV-07a)

1. The Banker arm (`cell-interactions` `bank/open.rs`) forwards a Team or Command click as `BankCellToBase::OrgVaultOpen` (ids from the cell entity, scope, Banker, distance, space) and logs `org_vault_open_requested`.
2. The base (`org_vault/open.rs`) finds the player's org of that type (unlocked; it only picks what to lock), runs `lock_actor`, and while holding the lock sends `onBagInfo` (every container; 17 at `bank_slots`, 19 at the Team's `vault_slots`, 20 at 100) then the vault's rows in one `onUpdateItem`, logs `org_vault_opened`, and sends `BankBaseToCell::OrgVaultGranted`.
3. The cell (`bank/org_open.rs::grant_org_vault`) re-checks the character, the pin and the range, records `VaultSession { scope, org_id: Some, .. }` and sends 107 or 108. Opening needs no bank bit (D-BV12: every member may look).

`VaultAccess::Open` and `VaultSession` gained `org_id`. `org_vault_refusal(container)` is `None` only for an open session of that container's scope naming an org; the session's `org_id` names the vault and authorizes nothing (every move re-reads membership under the lock). ORG-06's `OrgMembershipEnded` ends a session of that org with `vault_session_closed reason=org_left`.

### The moves (BV-07b)

`moveItem` routes to `move_/org/` before any lock when the target is 19/20 or the item is in the vault table (one primary-key read). The checks and their order are in `move_/org/mod.rs`'s module doc and `inventory-system.md` § "Moving items in and out of a Team or Command vault". The ones worth recording here:

- **Bits:** `DepositBank` into and within the vault, `WithdrawBank` out, both for a swap across (a deposit swap also withdraws the occupant, and the reverse).
- **No bound items** in a shared vault: another member could withdraw them, which would transfer a bound item. The personal vault allows bound items (D-BV08); this is stricter on purpose. Refused in Rust (`bound_item_not_org_storable`), with the table's CHECK behind it.
- **Entry rules otherwise match 17**: no mission items (the mission bag is the marker), `container_sets` must allow 17. The carried end must be 1-15.
- **Writes** are `INSERT ... SELECT` plus `DELETE` across the tables (every column travels in SQL; a whole move keeps its id; a split takes `nextval('sgw_inventory_item_id_seq')`). A cross swap parks the carried row at slot -1 (BV-03's sentinel, safe under the move lock).
- **Refusals** log `org_move_rejected`, send a line, and snap back under the move's lock order: the row from the player's bags, or from the vault only while the player is a member, or `onRemoveItem` when it is in neither. Every infrastructure abort is `move_failed` with the same line and snap-back (review S2).

### Fan-out gap

`broadcast_to_org` is not on main (ORG-07). Only the actor's client is updated. Another member with the window open keeps a stale view until they reopen it; dragging a stale item is refused `item_not_in_vault` and the item is removed from their window (`onRemoveItem`), from both the org path and the personal path's "source item not found" (only while an org session is open).

## Server-authority review (`server-authority-enforcer`, read-only)

Verdict: one blocker and three should-fixes, all fixed in BV-07b; no authorization bypass on the move itself.

- **B1, cross-org disclosure** (blocker): a refused verdict ran the snap-back with the organization the routing read found for the client's `item_id`, so a forged id with no session locked any org and received its vault row. Fixed: the refusal names the session's org or none, and the snap-back resends a vault row only while the player is a member. Guard: the second half of `moves::the_verdict_must_open_this_orgs_vault`.
- **S1, deadlock with vendor and trade**: fixed by the advisory-locks-first order above; `api.rs` corrected.
- **S2, silent infrastructure aborts**: now `move_failed` with a line and the snap-back.
- **S3, test gaps**: `move_bits.rs` (withdraw-only ranks swapping in or rearranging, a deposit swap without `WithdrawBank`, a bound item entering through a withdraw swap).
- **Nits:** a grant arriving after `OrgMembershipEnded` can open a session for an org the player has left; harmless (every move re-reads membership), recorded here. An unreachable `else { return; }` was folded into the verdict match.
- **Cleared:** membership and bits re-read under the lock on every move; forged ids, containers, slots and quantities; `useItem`/`removeItem` refuse 19/20; no trade, mail or vendor path reaches the vault table; the copy-and-delete writes; the cell grant's checks.

Its note is committed at `.claude/agent-memory/server-authority-enforcer/project_bank_vault_bv07_review.md`, annotated with the fixes.

## Known gaps

- **No fan-out to other members** until ORG-07's `broadcast_to_org` (see Design). Integration: once it lands, send the changed vault rows (and `onRemoveItem` for deleted ones) to every online member with that vault's session open, from `record::after_org_commit`.
- **World entry still declares 19 and 20 at 100.** The open re-sends `onBagInfo` with the Team's real size, which the client is expected to apply to the live window (BV-E1 Q2, inferred, not traced). UAT should confirm the Team window shows 40 slots after an open. Changing the world-entry `onBagInfo` would touch BV-05's `bank_slots` path, so it was left.
- **`onClearOrgVaultInventory` (74) is unused** (its container mapping is unknown); a client that left an org keeps the vault's rows in its cache until relog. They cannot be moved (every move re-checks membership).
- **The cell grant does not re-check membership**: the base checked it under the lock a moment earlier, and the session authorizes nothing by itself.
- **`move_/org/mod.rs` is 520 lines**, a little over the soft cap; `route` and the verdict steps are one concern with the orchestration.
- **In-game UAT is owed**: a Team and a Command Banker need seed rows (templates 370-389, spawns 470-489) and a test org; `.org_create` (ORG-05) makes the org.
