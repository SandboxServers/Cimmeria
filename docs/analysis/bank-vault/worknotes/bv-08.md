## Evidence

- **CM 19 never carries zero from the real client** (`Team.lua:186,195`, `if cashAmt > 0`). The decoder already refuses zero as `InvalidValue { field: "aCash" }`, which the cell router logged as malformed with no answer; the packet asked for a `zero_amount` reason, so that one case now gets `org_cash_rejected reason=zero_amount` and the refusal pair.
- **`sgw_player.naquadah` is `integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` with no CHECK**; `sgw_organizations.cash` is `bigint` with `CHECK (cash >= 0)`. The wallet therefore has no database backstop: its guards are the `UPDATE`'s WHERE and the cash log's non-negative CHECK.
- **`KEY SHARE` does not stop a plain `UPDATE` of `naquadah`** (a mail claim, a reward, org creation's debit), so the wallet balance read at lock time can be stale by the write (`database-persistence` consult).

## Design

### The log table (with `database-persistence`, read-only consult)

- **Chosen: a sibling table, `sgw_organization_cash_log`.** Columns: `org_id`, `org_type`, `account_id`, `player_id`, `rank`, `direction` (`deposit` \| `withdraw` \| `vault_expansion`), `amount bigint > 0`, `player_cash_before`/`after` (NULL only for `vault_expansion`), `org_cash_before`/`after`, `vault_slots_before`/`after` (only for `vault_expansion`), `tx_id`, `logged_at`. CHECKs pin the arithmetic per direction and the non-negative balances. No foreign keys (the trail outlives a disband and a character delete). Index `(org_id, logged_at)`.
- **Rejected: nullable item columns on `sgw_organization_vault_log` with a per-`kind` CHECK.** Fourteen of that table's columns describe an item and two slots and are `NOT NULL` for every row; a cash row has none of them, and its balances are `bigint` / `integer` where the vault log has `integer` stacks. The CHECK would carry the real rules for both shapes. A `ViewBankLogs` reader merges the two with `UNION ALL` ordered by `(logged_at, tx_id, log_id)` (the two sequences are separate, so `log_id` alone is not a global order).
- **From the consult:** `vault_slots_before`/`after` were added so a BV-09 debit says what it bought without a join; the wallet's before/after come from `RETURNING`, not the lock read; `$amt` is range-checked (it is a `u32` from `CashDir`, widened to `i64`); the treasury deposit has an SQL overflow guard so an overflow is a zero-row refusal, not a 22003 abort.

### The transaction (`org_cash/persist.rs`)

1. `SELECT account_id, naquadah FROM sgw_player WHERE player_id = $1 FOR KEY SHARE`; no row is `player_missing`.
2. `lock_org` (no row: `no_such_org`), then `member_access_locked` (no row: `not_a_member`). The bit for the direction: `DepositCash` or `WithdrawCash` (`no_permission`).
3. On the locked treasury: a withdrawal larger than `cash` is `insufficient_org_cash`; a deposit whose `checked_add` fails is `org_cash_overflow`.
4. The wallet: `UPDATE ... SET naquadah = naquadah - $2 WHERE ... AND naquadah >= $2 RETURNING naquadah` (deposit) or `+ $2 ... AND naquadah::bigint + $2 <= 2147483647` (withdraw). Zero rows: `insufficient_player_cash` or `player_cash_overflow`, with a re-read for the player's line.
5. The treasury: `cash - $2 WHERE cash >= $2` or `cash + $2 WHERE cash <= i64::MAX - $2`, `RETURNING cash` (unreachable misses under the lock, classified like step 3).
6. The log row, then the commit.

### After the commit (`org_cash/mod.rs`)

`broadcast_to_org` sends `onOrganizationCashUpdate` [48] with the new treasury to every online member (the actor included); the actor gets `onCashChanged` (75) with the new wallet and a line. The actor's sends find the session whose `active_player_id` is the actor and go through `send_to_current_player` (D-BV33).

### Refusals

Every refusal is one WARN `org_cash_rejected` with a stable `reason` and a line, except `actor_mismatch` (no session to tell). A wallet refusal resends the wallet; a treasury refusal resends the treasury to a member only. `no_such_org` and `not_a_member` share a line (review N1: no existence oracle). The reasons and lines are tabled in `organization-system.md` § "The treasury".

### No Banker check

The client shows the buttons only in the vault window, but CM 19 carries no vault verdict and the treasury is not the vault. Membership and the bit are the whole authorization; the server-authority review cleared this (fungible cash, no item rows, every withdrawal logged). Revisit only if location ever matters for cash.

## Server-authority review (`server-authority-enforcer`, read-only)

Verdict: conditional ship, no blockers; no route to duplicate, create or lose naquadah.

- **S1 (should-fix), no guard for a demote or kick landing while a transfer waits.** The code reads membership and bits after `lock_org`, but nothing pinned it. Added `race::a_demote_or_kick_that_lands_while_a_withdrawal_waits_is_seen` (the gate clears `WithdrawCash`, then removes the member, inside the lock-holding transaction).
- **S2 (should-fix), no D-BV33 recycled-entity guard.** Added `refusals::a_recycled_entity_id_moves_and_receives_nothing`.
- **N1, existence oracle:** `no_such_org` and `not_a_member` now share a line. Fixed.
- **N2, refusal shape differs by layer:** the cell's zero refusal sends `onErrorCode` plus a line (the org router's pair), the base's refusals a line only. Left as is; a playtest check that the vault window does not wait on `onErrorCode`.
- **N3, a negative wallet** (the column has no CHECK) turns a withdrawal into `query_failed` through the log's non-negative CHECK; the state is corrupt anyway and `error=` names it.
- **N4, no `log_id` / `tx_id` on the event:** the balances and the time join it to the row today.
- **N5, spam:** every refused transfer still takes the org row lock; a general org-surface rate limit, not BV-08's.
- **Cleared:** forged amounts (`i32::MIN`, zero, huge), overflow and negatives on both sides, forged org or entity ids, replay, two members racing, one player in a Team and a Command at once, the vendor and trade lock shapes, a character delete racing a transfer, disband racing a deposit (D-BV13 through `org_vault_is_empty`), feedback on every refusal, the telemetry, and no Banker check.

Its note is committed at `.claude/agent-memory/server-authority-enforcer/project_bank_vault_bv08_review.md`.
