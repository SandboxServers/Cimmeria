## Design

- **The trigger.** A GM-only `.orgvaultexpand [team|command] [from_slots]` next to `.bankexpand`. BV-05's quote/offer plumbing lives on the personal vault session and the Banker dialog, neither of which exists here, so the shape was adapted rather than reused: with no size the base **quotes** (size, price, treasury, and the exact command that buys), with the current size it **buys**, keyed on that size. The size is the replay key, as `from_slots` is in D-BV31: a repeated command, or two at once, buys once.
- **No vault session.** The purchase moves no item and the leader check under the lock is the authorization, so a GM need not stand at a Team Banker. `.bankexpand` requires a session because the personal verdict is its only proximity rule; here there is none to keep.
- **Scope.** `command` is accepted by the console and refused by the base under the lock (`command_vault_fixed`), so the refusal carries the Command's fields and the Command's treasury is provably untouched.
- **The transaction** (`expand.rs`): the unlocked membership lookup picks the org; `lock_actor` takes `FOR KEY SHARE` on the buyer, `lock_org` and `member_access_locked` (the BV-07 order; no advisory locks, no wallet write); a read of `vault_slots` and `cash` on the locked row; the checks in order `command_vault_fixed`, `not_leader`, `at_ceiling`, `price_missing` (a missing or zero price), then the quote, `replay`, `insufficient_org_cash`; then

  ```sql
  UPDATE sgw_organizations o
     SET vault_slots = o.vault_slots + 10, cash = o.cash - x.price_naquadah
    FROM resources.bank_expansion_price x
   WHERE o.org_id = $1 AND o.org_type = 1 AND o.vault_slots = $2
     AND o.vault_slots < 100 AND x.to_slots = o.vault_slots + 10
     AND x.price_naquadah = $3 AND o.cash >= x.price_naquadah
  RETURNING o.vault_slots, o.cash
  ```

  (a zero-row result is `row_changed`, unreachable under the lock), the BV-08 `insert_cash_log` row (`vault_expansion`, `player_cash: None`, `vault_slots: (from, from + 10)`), and the commit.
- **After the commit:** `onBagInfo` (`org_vault_bag_info(bank_slots, new size)`) and a line to the buyer through BV-08's `Actor` (addressed by `active_player_id`, D-BV33), and `onOrganizationCashUpdate` to every online member (`broadcast_to_org`).
- **The leader cannot be demoted.** `org_member_before_update` refuses any rank change to or from 8 outside the member-delete trigger's promotion, so the only way to lose leadership mid-purchase is to leave; that race is `not_a_member`.

## Server-authority review (`server-authority-enforcer`, read-only)

Verdict: conditional ship, no blockers. No lying client or GM can spend a treasury it does not lead, charge twice, grow past 100, buy free, or drive the treasury negative.

- **S1 (should-fix), no guard for leadership lost while the purchase waits.** A direct demotion is impossible (`org_member_before_update` refuses a rank change to or from 8), so the guard is the reachable form: `refusals::a_leader_who_leaves_while_the_purchase_waits_buys_nothing` (the gate deletes the leader's member row, the trigger promotes the other member, the purchase is `not_a_member`).
- **S2 (should-fix), no D-BV33 recycled-entity guard.** Added `refusals::a_recycled_entity_id_receives_nothing`.
- **N1, the not-in-org line said "Team" for `command`.** Fixed: the line names the scope; asserted in `no_team_and_database_failures_are_refused`.
- **N2, no `price_missing` test.** Documented in the test module (shared seed rows).
- **N3, the buyer's `bank_slots` in the `onBagInfo` can be a step stale** if a personal `.bankexpand` commits in between; cosmetic.
- **N4, other members' open Team window keeps the old size** until reopened (they get the treasury, not an `onBagInfo`, which would have to carry each recipient's own `bank_slots`). Known gap below.
- **N5, "leader only" does not bind a GM,** who can make themselves leader with the org GM commands; the `expand` row (`gm_override = true`) and the cash log row are the audit trail.
- **Cleared:** client-supplied fields (only `scope` and `from_slots`), targeting another Team, double purchase and replay, grow-only and the ceiling, the price, the treasury, the Command vault, the lock order against BV-07 moves and BV-08 transfers, the transaction, the replies, the telemetry.

## Known gaps
