## 0. Headline findings

1. **Respawn (`ReanchorPlayer`) silently breaks the client for the rest of the session.** Char 71 died at 00:37:11 UTC and respawned at 00:37:25 via `reanchor_player.rs` (CREATE_BASE_PLAYER burst + appearance + tint + inventory resync, nothing else). From that second until logoff 28 minutes later the client sent **zero** `triggerClientHintedGenericRegion` calls (it had sent them steadily before; in the same 28 minutes it still sent 21 interacts and scored 4 kills). That is why the Throne Room "doesn't recognise me" (706 step 2411 is a client-hinted region trigger), and is the most likely cause of "mission log broken" at 7:47 PM and possibly of the lingering death heartbeat. The Jaffa never died and got `Castle.ThroneRoom` on the first pass.
2. **Take-cover objective (639 / objective 2484) is an edge-trigger race, not a timer.** `player_entered_cover` fires on proximity *enter* to cover_set 1381 (1 Hz tick, no crouch check). The Ambernol vial sits on that desk, so the enter edge fires ~1 s *before* chain 1032 activates step 2144 (`no chains matched`), and never re-fires until the player walks out of the radius and back. Reproduced identically on both characters.
3. **End state is a server gap, not tester error.** After the DHD Livewire repair, the DHD carries `INT_DHD` (16) but `interactions/dispatch/interact.rs` has no arm for it (`interact: target has no static interaction type`, 76 clicks), and `add_dialog_set 3073` resolves `dialog_id=None` (the seed comment in `castle_706_708_chains.sql` already calls this INERT pending CA02). No dial request ever reaches the server.
4. **We do not have the tester's client logs.** No `launcher.*` target, no dev-session call, and the `session_id` key does not exist in SigNoz at all. Only server-side telemetry exists for this session.
5. XP: 47 grants, all `Granting kill XP` at a flat 10 each. Zero mission XP (every mission in the chain has `reward_xp = 0` in `missions.sql`).
6. Several tester complaints are **documented, deliberate seed decisions** (boot lock is a no-op; no Mess Hall dialog; armory optional objective is cosmetic; crate has only two loot variants; 1521 Symbiote deferred). They are confirmed by logs but are scope decisions, not regressions.
