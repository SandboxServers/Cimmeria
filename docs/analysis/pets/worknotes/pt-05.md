## Design decisions

1. **Pre-pass, not a new `AiState`.** `pet::pre_pass` runs inside each NPC's outcome slot, before the state match. For a non-pet it returns the snapshot state untouched. For a pet it returns the state to run, or `None` when it used the turn itself (a teleport, or an owner who is unavailable).
   - It reads the pet's **current** state, not the dispatcher's pre-loop snapshot. A mob that ran earlier in the same tick can have preempted the pet into Fighting, and re-arming Follow on the stale snapshot left a Follow pet with a live threat list. Found by an order-dependent test failure under nextest.
2. **Follow.** A pet out of a fight is always `Follow` with `follow_target_id = owner` and the band set to 2/5. `idle_is_ticked` admits every pet, so a fresh (Idle) pet is ticked at all. PT-01 left it admitted to the query but filtered out by the Idle rule.
3. **Teleport** (D-PT07): when the pet is more than 40 u from the owner horizontally, or more than 4 u off the owner's height.
   - It is rate-limited on `PetState::last_teleport_at`, which PT-02 also stamps.
   - The move is PT-02's `on_owner_teleported(owner, OwnerPath::PetLeftBehind, ..)`: the same grounded spot, an immediate `EntityMoved` and the `owner_teleported` row. PT-05 logs only the decision (why, and how far).
   - Side effect: an owner with several pets gets all of them moved.
4. **Leash anchored on the owner** (A-28): `fight_target::select_target` builds `Engagement.spawn_pos` from `pet::leash_anchor`, which is the owner's position for a pet and `spawn_position` otherwise. `leash_trigger` itself is unchanged, with the same radius, hysteresis and vertical cap.
5. **Re-arm after a fight** (A-29): `begin_leash` branches first for a pet into `rearm_after_fight`. That drains, clears threat, releases cover, arms Follow and syncs the owner's combat. There is no walk home, no evade and no heal, unlike NA42's follower path, which heals on arrival.
   - `purge_dead_player_from_threat` and the pre-pass's Leashing arm reach the same branch.
6. **Stances** (D-PT09):
   - **Passive:** `generate_threat` refuses threat to a Passive pet, before the Leashing arm, so there is no preempt and no threat. A pet switched to Passive mid-fight re-arms on its next turn.
   - **Defensive:** engages a mob that is Fighting with the owner or the pet on its list, within 40 u of the owner. A hit still preempts through `generate_threat`.
   - **Aggressive:** also takes the owner's `current_target_id` once the owner has `BSF_InCombat` (hostile, within 40 u of the owner). It also scans for hostile NPCs within 15 u of the pet, inside the 4 u vertical band and not LoS-blocked.
   - Engagement seeds 1.0 threat through `generate_threat(mob -> pet, AggroCause::PetStance)`. `PetStance` never recruits assisters and maps to transition reason `pet_engage`.
   - Candidates are `npc_ids_in_space_of`, which is SGWMob-only, so a pet never targets a player, another pet or a being.
7. **No engagement while left behind:** past the teleport distance or off the owner's floor, the stance picks nothing. Advisor finding: without this the pet re-engaged and re-leashed every turn during the 5 s teleport cooldown.
8. **Targets a fighting pet lets go:**
   - a mob that is Leashing, Despawning or Dead (`target_resetting`);
   - a mob inside its post-reset re-aggro window (`target_just_reset`);
   - a mob more than 40 u from the owner or off its floor (`target_far_from_owner`).

   This stops the pet re-pulling a mob that has reset (advisor finding). It also covers PT-02's hand-off: after an owner teleport, a fighting pet follows instead of running back.
9. **Owner combat (D-PT06):** `defend::sync_owner_combat` runs on every pet turn and on re-arm.
   - Every live mob with the pet on its threat list enters the owner's `threatened_mobs` through `enter_player_combat`. On the edge it sends an appearance refresh, then `onStateFieldUpdate` to self and witnesses (the `damage_apply` order).
   - An owner entry that neither the owner nor any of the owner's pets explains on that mob's threat list leaves again, and the owner alone is sent `onStateFieldUpdate`, as the leash's `send_combat_exits` does.
   - `clear_dead_npc_from_all_player_threat` now shares the drain's walk, which also covers players whose `threatened_mobs` names the mob. Without it the owner stayed in combat after its pet's kill.
10. **Never hostile to players:** `is_hostile_to_players` is false for any entity with `PetState`, whatever its faction or override.
11. **Ability choice:** both selector functions skip `PetState::toggled_off`. With everything toggled off the pet holds fire, as an all-cooling NPC does.
12. **PT-06 overlap:** none. The pet fires abilities only through `fight.rs`, which PT-06 routes through the kill-credit wrapper. PT-05 does not edit `fight.rs`.

## Known gaps

- **Owner combat lags by up to one AI tick.** The mirror runs on the pet's 2 s turn, so the owner's `BSF_InCombat` can follow the pet's first hit by up to 2 s. Only `damage_apply` could make it immediate, and that file is PT-06's.
- **A dismissed or despawned pet leaves entries.** Any owner entry that pet's fight put in `threatened_mobs` stays until that mob dies or leashes; nothing re-runs `sync_owner_combat`. PT-02's `despawn_pet` could run an owner-scoped reconcile.
- **The exit edge goes to the owner only.** `owner_combat_left` sends `onStateFieldUpdate` to the owner alone, matching the existing leash exit path; the entry edge goes to self and witnesses. Witnesses therefore do not see the bit clear.
- **Hostile mobs still engage a pet only after it hits them** (A-30): there is no proximity aggro or assist on a pet. That is the decided scope.
- **A teleport moves every pet of the owner.** Harmless with one pet per owner. A second pet that is fighting drops its target on its next turn (`target_far_from_owner`).
- **A content `generate_threat` aimed at a Passive pet is refused.** This is documented in `pet-system.md`.
- **No kill-credit wiring here.** That is PT-06, through `fight.rs`.
