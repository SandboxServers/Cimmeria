## Design decisions

1. **Two choke points, one line per site.** `on_owner_left(owner, reason, tx, mgr)` and `on_owner_teleported(owner, tx, mgr)` hold every rule; each A-31 site adds one call. `forget_owner` (PT-01's disconnect hook) is now `on_owner_left(.., OwnerDisconnected)`. Both are a single map lookup for a player without pets, so player-only paths call them unconditionally.
2. **Despawn before the owner's `destroy_entity`.** An owner that is the last player in an instanced space takes the space down with it; the pet has to go through `despawn_npc` first so its witnesses get `LeftAoI`. On the transfer paths (gate, space transfer, gmGotoLocation) the call sits after the confirmed `GateTravel` send, beside the trade cancel, so a failed send still changes nothing.
3. **The owner gets no `LeftAoI` when its own view is being torn down** (`PetDespawnReason::owner_view_torn_down`: `OwnerDisconnected`, `OwnerGone`, `OwnerLeftSpace`). The traveller's client is reset by `RESET_ENTITIES`; a `LeftAoI` queued behind `GateTravel` would be held by the base until the new world's `onClientReady` and then flushed for an entity the new world never had. `despawn_pet` scrubs the pet from the owner's witness set before `despawn_npc`, which reads that set. Every other witness still gets it. On owner death (`OwnerDead`) the owner is told. This also closes PT-01's "owner gets a LeftAoI for its own pet on disconnect" gap. The aoi-witness-broadcast advisor checked the base side and found no per-client bookkeeping left stale by the skipped leave.
4. **Same-space move** puts each live pet 2 u behind the owner (`beside_owner`, the summon offset), walked there with `move_along_navmesh` from the owner's feet: it stops at walls and takes the floor height (the owner's own Y can be a GM coordinate or a ring pad origin). No mesh, or the owner off it: the raw spot. Then `snap_npc_from(.., MoveSource::PetTeleport)` (grid-consistent write, path cleared, velocity zero, facing = the owner's), `last_teleport_at` stamped, and an `EntityMoved` (zero velocity) to each witness in the pet's last-tick witness set, the same relay the content `move_waypoint` uses. The next AoI tick sends Left/Entered for range changes. A dead pet is not moved. A pet found in another space is despawned (`OwnerLeftSpace`).
5. **Ordering of the move.** Every caller queues it after the owner's own snap (`TeleportPlayer`, or `ReanchorPlayer` on the same-world respawn, per the aoi advisor). On the same-world ring the move happens at `Effect::ShowPlayer`, when the owner reappears at the destination, not at `Effect::TeleportPlayer`, when the owner is moved while still hidden and movement-locked (movement advisor finding). An abort release also emits `ShowPlayer`; the owner did not move then, and the pet just closes up beside it.
6. **Corpse timer in the sweep, not in `resolve_death`.** The sweep stamps `despawn_at = now + 10 s` the first tick it sees a dead pet with a live owner, and despawns it (`CorpseExpired`) once that passes. That keeps every pet rule in `pets/` and leaves the death resolver untouched apart from the owner-death call, at the cost of up to one tick (100 ms) on the timer. `pet_owner_sweep_at(now, ..)` makes it testable without sleeping. A living pet with a `despawn_at` (none today) expires with `Expired`.
7. **Owner death is immediate** from `resolve_death`'s player branch, after the Defeat Window and the threat purge. The sweep's `OwnerDead` rule stays as the backstop.
8. **Scope additions** beyond the packet's list, same bug class, one line each: `gmGoto`, `gmGotoXYZ`, `gmSummon` (player target) and `.location` also move the pet. The movement validator's snap-back (`base_messages/movement.rs`) is exempt: it returns the player to where it already was.
9. **Wiring guard.** `every_owner_travel_site_calls_the_pet_hooks` scans the production source of the seven cell crates: a file that sends `CellToBaseMsg::GateTravel` must call `pets::on_owner_left(`, one that sends `CellToBaseMsg::TeleportPlayer` must call `pets::on_owner_teleported(` (snap-back exempt). It also asserts it found at least 6 and 5 such files, so it cannot pass vacuously.

## Known gaps

- **Pet combat state on a teleport.** The move stops the pet and clears its path but leaves `ai_state` and any threat or target alone; a Fighting pet may path back toward its old target on the next AI tick. Stance, follow and owner-relative leash are PT-05, which should also reuse `on_owner_teleported` (or `grounded_spot_behind`) for its 40 u follow teleport so there is one teleport routine.
- **Same-world ring abort.** An aborted trip emits `ShowPlayer` for an owner that never moved; the pet then snaps beside the owner. Harmless, but it is a move the player did not ask for.
- **`EntityMoved` over a long jump.** Whether the client snaps or interpolates an NPC across a large `EntityMoved` delta is not verified in game (the content `move_waypoint` and the NPC respawn use the same relay). If UAT shows the pet sliding across the map, the fix is a Left/Entered pair for witnesses beyond a threshold, in `on_owner_teleported` only.
- **gmGotoLocation does not cancel an open trade** before its destroy, unlike the other transfer paths (pre-existing, out of scope; noted for the coordinator).
- The `SGWPet.def` cell methods `onOwnerDeath` / `onOwnerLeash` / `onOwnerRespawn` stay unused; the cell calls the hooks directly.
