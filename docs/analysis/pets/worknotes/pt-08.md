## Design decisions

1. **Redirect keyed on scripts.** An ability is an owner-pet ability when one of its effects has `PetStatBuff`, `PetDeathTimer` or `HealPetHealth`. The data names it; no ability-id table.
2. **Target discarded, pet from the registry.** Like the summon (decision 23), `target_id` becomes 0 at launch, so #444 is untouched. `SpaceManager::owner_pet_targets` keeps a pet only when `summoner_matches`, alive, same space. Never the client's id, never a bare owner id.
3. **State on the pet.** `PetBuff` ledger with the applied deltas; removal takes back exactly that. Bounds widen instead of clamping (deviation from python, recorded in the ADR).
4. **Toggle.** `AF_TOGGLED` ability: on, then off. Chat line with the new state.
5. **To The Death.** 4121 = timed +400 buff, 4119 arms `doomed_at`, 4122 unscripted (the tick carries it out). Kill: `kill_npc_out_of_band(pet, pet, false, grant_xp = false)` after HEALTH 0. Re-cast refused while doomed (133): python's refresh would make it permanent (advisor).
6. **Heed Our Calling.** The brief asked for the stat to change, so the effect writes `speedPet = base + 100` through `effects::passives::apply_passives` at `InitPlayerState`, `AbilityGranted`, `AbilitiesReset` (remove). The advisor preferred deriving it at read time in `effective_warmup`; I followed the brief. The stat is left non-dirty (server-side only), so no existing burst changes.
7. **Lord's Concentration (D-PT17 PROPOSED).** New server-only effect 350: +50 `interruptRes` for 30 s on every owner pet. The movement-interrupt alternative was rejected (unevidenced exception to decision 21).
8. **Heal magnitudes.** 3211 = 20% (the effect row; the tooltip says 15%), 3230 = 5% × 15, 3350 = 10% × 10. 1214 revive not wired. 1646 not redirected (universal starter, D-AT09).
9. **Scripts in `pet_scripts.rs`,** not `scripts.rs` (1648 lines, over the hard cap).
