- **Command targeting:** `cell-console/.../dispatch.rs::resolve_target` requires a selected player for `Target::Player` specs, and `registry/commands/progression.rs` declares `.allcraft`, `.craftkit`, `.learnblueprint`, `.learndiscipline` and `.forgetdiscipline` as `Target::Player`, so none falls back to the caller.
- **Rolls:** research of 5481 with discipline 21 at expertise 1 and one kicker has chance `100 − 1 + 5 = 104`, so step 12 always succeeds and teaches blueprint 1 (discipline 21 known). Discipline 22 is unknown, so 21 is the only eligible one.
- **Step 9:** the spend checks ASP before the paradigm and the paradigm before prerequisites, so with ASP above 0 the refusal line moves from the paradigm to the prerequisite once the Goa'uld guide (paradigm 3) is used.
- **Numbers:** `python tools/docs-gen/regen.py --check --skip-crate-graph` (exit 1, as expected with stale blocks) reports the generated totals would become 486 / CW 167 / NT 121 / IM 101 / KM 94 / NU 3, which matches the "Since" row and the project-status headline. Its output was not committed.
