## Evidence contract

Every mission and encounter packet uses one of three provenance classes **per story beat**, not just per quest. **`RESTORED_SOURCE`** means an exact 2009 mission, step, objective, dialog or asset relation supports that beat; the packet cites its file, ID and build when known. **`CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION`** means the historical destination or goal is supported but a missing placement, transition or mechanic has been rebuilt to make it playable; the packet states which part is inferred and seeks a source-compatible design review. The staged world-19 Curia approach is in this class. **`CIMMERIA_AUTHORING`** means a new quest, character exchange or activity created for the living MMO; it receives a new ID, lore review and an explicit authored label. A reused historical NPC or prop does not turn a new story into restoration. No packet may call a reconstructed or authored beat a recovered 2009 fact, and a missing build lineage keeps the affected beat held. Each future ledger records the class, source citation, remaining uncertainty and approving advisor before coder dispatch.

The supplied `00_FINAL_SUMMARY.txt` reports 24 recovered world dossiers. The matching `SGW_FINAL_DEVELOPER_HANDOFF.zip` was found in Downloads and has the four-zone CSV/dossier set. Its master handoff describes `SGW_FINAL_RE.sqlite` and `SGW_ALL_FINDINGS_ARCHIVE.sqlite`, but neither database was provided. The owner confirmed this omission; their absence is an evidence limit, not a planning blocker. Treat the included CSV rows as source-backed at their stated build and relation, not as proof of currently playable server behavior. Source-name VO, UI and asset associations remain candidates. Older `docs/content/zone-audit.md` and `docs/content/external-data-analysis.md` contain historical snapshot counts; compare against the current seed and later campaign work before treating a gap as open.

## Packet sizing and dispatch gate

A hook caps Haiku workers at **90k tokens of total context**, and about 35–40k of that is fixed startup overhead, so each Haiku implementation packet targets **about 40k working tokens** (`docs/analysis/campaign-metrics/findings.md`, "Packet sizing against the 90k Haiku cap", and its README). Owner decision, 2026-10-13: the coordinator checks every packet against this budget **at seal time**, when its allowlist, IDs and tests are frozen; a packet whose sealed contract would exceed it splits into children before dispatch. No *new* pre-splits were added for this budget decision; the existing mandatory splits stand (Harset's eight split families → 22 children, Tollana T-08 → two children and T-10 → one child per mission). Older "50–60k" figures in the zone files predate this decision and are superseded for Haiku workers. Specialist (archaeology, design, UAT) packets are not bound by the Haiku cap but should still stop and split rather than overrun.

Packets likely to need a split at seal time, because each already bundles several independent graphs: Tollana T-03 (568–570) and T-08 (612–619, already split in two), Castle CS-05 (service plus repeatables), Harset HA-04 (1343 and 1347) and HA-09 (1377 and 1374).

Before scaling to larger implementation waves, the [execution contract](execution-contract.md#measured-pilot-before-larger-waves) requires one evidence packet and one small implementation packet to be completed and measured. Their ledger worknotes record actual tokens, elapsed time, tests, review effort and unresolved UAT. Treat the 65 families as a scope map, not a schedule. After CB-N05a merges, the first authored quest is a bounded live-client catalog proof; further authored-quest implementation waits for that proof. If the owner has not approved a lab lease, keep that work `UATPending` and continue independent restoration packets.

The packet documents give source-backed behavior, files, tests, branches and stop rules. Specialist gate packets must freeze unresolved IDs, coordinates, build lineage, client catalog entries, exact dialog/copy and item grants into a signed manifest before the matching Haiku packet becomes Ready. Use one worker per worktree and test database. Merge packet commits into a campaign feature branch; run local CI-equivalent checks and fresh adversarial review at each wave, send accepted fixes to a separate fixer and the repaired diff to a new reviewer, for at most three rounds or until no issue remains. Block unresolved findings. Open one remote PR for the **complete campaign** only after its final local CI and review pass, then wait for remote CI and the merge queue. The one exception is CB-N05a, which ships early as its own infrastructure PR. The four investigations and packet writing do not start implementation.

The likely shared sequence is: reconcile sources and old campaign states; survey and approve placement; restore original faction chains; populate service and ambient loops; add approved authored content; then run cross-zone client UAT. Each zone close-out updates its own ledger; FZ-99 then updates the shared status docs once for all four zones. A zone's first packet must remove stale assumptions from its old ledger before later packets can be called Ready.
