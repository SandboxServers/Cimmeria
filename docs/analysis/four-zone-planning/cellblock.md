## Phase 2 — Intent and content density

**Recovered intent:** a controlled escape that introduces movement, body search, combat, NPC dialog, Livewire, Ambernol, ring travel, cover, escort and class gear before Castle. Evidence: the original mission/space scripts cited in `docs/content/mission-chains.md`, seeded mission steps, and the world-12 spawn sequence. The scripted `StraegisAttack` camera beat is confirmed by QA map exports 953, 281, 988 and 89 in `docs/analysis/cellblock-asset-audit/evidence-ledger.md` E08–E10, and `docs/analysis/cellblock-asset-audit/knowledge-gaps.md`; successful visual playback remains a separate question.

**Project design opportunity:** add life without inflating the prison with unrelated quest hubs. The space is instanced, linear, under attack, and already combat heavy. Good candidate additions are (a) bounded overheard NID/prisoner dialogue and purposeful stationary or short-patrol noncombat cast in safe pockets; (b) one or two optional evidence/rescue interactions that use existing geometry and end before the final ring; (c) better combat/cover staging and recovery, preserving existing mission order. These are proposals, **not recovered 2009 content**. Do not add random repeatable kill/fetch quests or extra enemies to the wake-up and med-bay tutorial until route difficulty is observed. The orphan mission 642 is marked skeleton/dead content in `docs/content/mission-chains.md`; its label alone does not justify activation. Frost's Letter 1360 already supplies a light optional narrative thread into Castle. Mission 688 already extends the route beyond 687.

**Owner direction received during planning:** restore documented 2009 behavior first, then allow lore-safe new quests. Across the four-zone program, “alive” includes core story, services, hubs, ambient density and repeatable activity where pacing supports it. The owner has explicitly ruled out repeatables in Cellblock: keep the escape paced and one-time. Optional side content may be authored after restoration, but it must end within the route or hand off cleanly to Castle.

## Owner interview / decisions before implementation

1. **Content identity:** Owner direction is settled: restore original behavior first, then consider clearly labelled, lore-safe Cimmeria-original quests. Confirm the first optional quest outline and reward before N05; quantity remains open.
2. **Pacing:** How long should a first character spend here, and how much detour is acceptable before Castle? The documented 37-minute run included five deaths; that is an observed session, not a target average.
3. **Population:** Should extra life be mostly voiced/text ambient cast, or do you want more combatants? Proposed default: ambience first, no more forced kills until difficulty UAT.
4. **Narrative:** If optional quests are approved, should they be NID/prisoner lore, aid to fellow captives, evidence for Castle, or class-specific moments? Confirm any archetype restrictions and reward ceiling.
5. **Preservation:** For mission 642 and unbound dialog/VO candidates, should unrecovered ideas remain archival until exact wiring is found, or can they seed explicitly new authoring? Never label a new chain as original.
6. **Presentation ownership:** Shall the already planned Cellblock asset-audit and ring campaigns keep their visual/exit work, with this campaign focused on playability/population/content? Proposed default: yes.
7. **Repeatables:** Resolved by owner: none in Cellblock. The campaign keeps its one-time escape pacing; any repeatable related to these characters belongs in another zone's planning.
