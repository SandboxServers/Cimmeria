## Phase 2 — reconstructed intent and design limits

Harset is a shared three-faction hub (world 57), a shared Command Center (68), and per-player Market and Storage spaces (69/70), as documented in `audit.md` against `entities/spaces.xml`. The five ring switches and the Command Center door come from surviving Python; all-to-all ring routing is supported by `db/resources/Events/Seed/point_sets.sql` and the recovered `935–939` ring EventSet rows in `04_KISMET_EVENTSET_ACTORS.csv`. The mission catalog is much broader than what the original Harset Python can execute. The earlier campaign selected 36 current missions and cross-zone carry-ins after distinguishing older revisions and offworld content (`docs/analysis/harset-rebuild/README.md`, decisions D-H01/D-H02). The owner directed a fresh review of all 73 ZIP-linked records before any implementation. Classify each against historical build, faction, map, current seed, dialog, chain and progression evidence; retain, exclude or hold each with a written reason. The review can preserve the 36-mission selection, but cannot assume it.

For new life, keep three classes separate: **restoration** of a proven script/seed edge, **reconstruction** of an unscripted historical mission from its step/dialog/item evidence, and **new authoring** for the MMO feel. A map object's location is evidence that the object existed, not evidence that a quest used it. The ZIP's empty encounter CSVs give no license to invent exact 2009 spawn composition. The owner chose **restore the core first, then add lore-safe authored quests**; “alive” includes core story, services, hubs, ambient density and repeatable activity. Build those in that order. Do not use old-revision missions such as 571–574 as current quest IDs merely because `02_MISSIONS.csv` maps them to Harset; first compare the build, step catalog, existing IDs, and story order.
