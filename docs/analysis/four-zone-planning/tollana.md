## Proposed experience

Make Tollana a contested hub with legible districts: gate/base camp; repair facility and data center; Travell's camp; internment camp; Goa'uld resort and Jaffa plaza; black market; Curia approach. Populate each with cast, guards, civilians, vendors and short ambient interactions at navmesh-checked positions. Preserve the recovered faction arcs first. The owner wants all four paths planned: OPC technology (568–570), Sagrine rescue (590), black market (598), Lyala investigation/data (601–602); Goa'uld resort/Beleth (604–611); DRK Book of the Gates/Curia (612–621); and Loyalist Jaffa contact/Asmodai (1626–1723). Mission 601 ends with Harta receiving findings; the separate DRK mission 879 is the Lyala return. Missions 603, 611 and 618 have explicit Harset steps and require the Harset campaign's handoff contract. Side missions 743–744, 860–882 and 1301 are separate candidates, not automatic additions. The owner chose a staged world-19 area for the Curia climax rather than the stub world-88 map.

Only after these arcs are playable, plan about 5–8 authored local stories plus repeatables across the hub: repair salvage, camp supply, black-market delivery, civilian evacuation and faction-specific follow-ups. Start rewards with useful supplies and modest XP/currency. Mark these `RECONSTRUCTION` in the content ledger; settle exact lore, faction eligibility and reset rules before packet dispatch. Avoid invented Curia politics or a fabricated council interior.

## Decisions and blockers

1. **Curia decision resolved**: stage the climax in world 19. The current world-88 stub does not support a council scene. Exact world-19 location and set dressing remain T-01 design tasks.
2. **Faction scope resolved**: plan OPC, Goa'uld, Loyalist Jaffa and DRK paths. Whether level-1 and side records should be reconstructed remains an evidence decision. The 51 rows are a census, not 51 automatic implementation commitments. T-00 must freeze build lineage and per-ID dispositions before those rows enter a coding packet.
3. **Cross-zone ownership**: the Harset ZIP lists 1364/1366 because their monikers are Harset; their objective text explicitly uses Tollana. Conditional T-05a/b own each complete chain on the Tollana feature branch, including the Harset source/grant and Tollana recipient test. Harset HA-00b records this interface and does not duplicate it. Missions 603/611/618 and three Loyalist missions also cross into Harset and need receiving-side tests.
4. **Authored life**: owner wants core story, services, hubs, ambient density, about 5–8 lore-safe side stories and repeatable activity, after restoration. Rewards start with useful supplies and modest XP/currency. Individual job concepts and whether ambient civilians may use recovered names without exact placement evidence still need content review.
5. **Source archive**: the handoff author did not provide the SQLite databases claimed by the master handoff. Packet T-00 must track each claim to available CSV/repo rows and mark missing joins as unresolved; no packet depends on receiving the databases.

No player-facing behavior should be called proven until mission, dialog, placement and client behavior are reconciled separately. Runtime behavior packets need the test types in `TESTING.md`, seed reload per `seed-change`, doc updates per `docs/agents/doc-update-map.md`, and compiling checks through the build lane.
