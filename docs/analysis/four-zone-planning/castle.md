## Phase 2 — intent and the experience gap

The original mission texts point to a prison uprising with three parallel pressures: rescue Zuritska, seize medical supplies for stasis-sick prisoners, and use the Symbiote Chamber for the Jaffa branch. Current population makes those rooms look occupied, but the infirmary and chamber have no quest verbs. The most credible content expansion is to finish these *shipped* branches, then add restrained, clearly labeled Cimmeria side quests that reuse local actors and spaces. The core campaign should avoid reopening completed 701-708 chains unless UAT proves a defect.

Existing UAT risk is more important than raw NPC count. The old client playtest saw two simultaneous Zuritskas, a follower stopped 50-95 units behind, and Romney placed in a sealed wing; follow-up fixes and navigation need a fresh walking pass (`docs/analysis/playtests/2026-09-18-colo-castle/README.md:88-90,117`; `docs/analysis/castle-population/README.md:80`). The Castle navmesh has distinct connected components: indoor/field 250, checkpoint/bunker 116, and isolated Symbiote Chamber 468. A new quest must provide a reachable route or explicit ring handoff, not simply put an objective marker on another island. The 24/36 population already supplies ambient life; add named NPCs only where a quest needs a distinct speaker, service, or repeatable loop. Any further fighting population needs local aggro and respawn safety checks against ring pads, respawners and mission actors (`docs/analysis/castle-population/README.md:D-CP04-D-CP08`).
