## Evidence

- **1445 "EMP Ammunition"**: "Buff: / Toggle / Damage Type: Physical / Penetration: Decreased / Damage: Increased", `effect_ids` empty, no `resources.effects` row names it. The text is word-for-word Hollow Point's (715), so it gives only directions.
- **EMP Grenade (ability 2864)** is the one EMP the cooked data describes in full: effect 4202 "Non-Mechanical Target Damage -100F / -0H", effect 4200 "Mechanical Target Damage -0F / -225H", effect 4201 "Disorient" (20 s), split by the "Mechanical Type Check" effects 4203 / 4204. Overload Mine (1142, effects 1297 / 1298 / 4217) makes the same split. EMP drains a living target's Focus and damages a machine's Health, never both.
- **EMP Grenades ammo effect 3520** (ability 2422): "Ammunition Type: EMP / Damage Type: Energy / DOT: -50 F -5 H / 10 Ticks". Not used: it is the grenade launcher's ammo, not a bullet, and a 10-tick DoT on every shot would be far from "modest".

## How the server tells machines apart

There is no mechanical flag anywhere the server loads: `EEntityFlags` (`ref_MOB_FLAGS`), `entity_templates` and `EFaction` have none, and the grenade's "Mechanical Type Check" effects carry no NVPs or script. The body set is the signal: `CellEntity::body_set` comes from `entity_templates.body_set` at spawn. `MECHANICAL_BODY_SETS` holds five prefixes, chosen by name:

| Prefix | Templates |
|---|---|
| `MOB_CA_DroneTank.` | Prisoner Retrieval Unit (templates 4 and 145) |
| `MOB_Goauld_Drone.` | Malfunctioning Drone |
| `MOB_AncientDrone.` | Ancient Drone |
| `MOB_BattleWalker.` | BattleWalker |
| `WP-Human.BS_Deployable` | Deployables (Microwave Emitter and the other `BS_Deployable*` sets) |

A player is never mechanical, whatever its body set.

## Changes from the plan

1. **A new script, not an existing one.** `RangedEnergyDamage` with only `FocusDamage` would drain every target alike; the split needs the target's body set, so `EmpDisrupt` is new.
2. **No disable, interrupt or disorient.** The grenade disorients machines for 20 s; the rounds do not. `Stun` would need `pulse_count > 1`, which re-runs `on_apply` (a second drain) each pulse and leaks the `BSF_MOVEMENT_LOCK` refcount on refresh (the coordinator's AM-11a finding), and NPC AI does not read `BSF_MOVEMENT_LOCK` anyway. Interrupting a cast or warmup lives in `cimmeria-cell-combat`, which an effect script in `cimmeria-cell-world` cannot reach. Effect 9120 is single-shot (`pulse_count` 1): no registration, no stacking, nothing to remove.
3. **One extra shared line.** The pipeline tests are their own file, so `damage_apply/mod.rs` gets one `#[cfg(test)] mod ammo_emp_tests;` line next to AM-04's `ammo_tests`.

## Telemetry

`ammo_emp_disrupt` (DEBUG, target `ammo`), once per EMP hit: `entity_id` and `player_id` of the shooter, `target_entity_id`, `target_template_id`, `effect_id`, `mechanical`, `focus_before`, `focus_drained`, `health_before`, `health_damage`; `reason=target_missing` when the target is gone. It needs a catalog row in work-packets.md and a constant in `ammo_telemetry.rs` through the coordinator, like AM-04's `ammo_on_hit_effect_missing`.

## Known limits

- **Penetration is dead in live play.** `MITIGATION` is capped at 0 in the default stats, so `penetration_mult` 0.75 does nothing until mitigation is populated; EMP rounds are a plain 10% damage raise plus the on-hit effect until then.
- **Focus is the shield.** `RangedPhysicalDamage` lets Focus absorb a shot before Health, so draining a living target's Focus makes the next shots bleed into Health sooner. EMP adds no Health damage against a living target by itself.
- **Health from `EmpDisrupt` can kill.** `damage_apply`'s effect-driven death sweep runs after the on-hit script, so the 5 Health taken from a machine at 5 HP or less produces a normal kill.

## Client exposure and UAT

Effect 9120 is not in the client's cooked data, so it must never reach the client by id (unknown cooked ids have crashed the client before, #938). It does not:

- It is single-shot (`pulse_count` 1), so `register_active_effect` never runs for it: no `onTimerUpdate`, no buff icon.
- The shot's `onEffectResults` carries the ability id and the shot's sequence number, not the on-hit effect id.
- `EmpDisrupt` only writes stats; `damage_apply` flushes them with the ordinary `onStatUpdate` (stat ids and values).

The live check for AM-12's UAT: fire EMP rounds at a Castle Prisoner Retrieval Unit and at a Jaffa guard with `ammo.finite_special` on, and confirm in SigNoz one `ammo_emp_disrupt` row per hit (`mechanical=true` / `false`), no client error, and the target's Focus bar (living) or Health bar (drone) dropping by the seeded amount on top of the shot.

## Follow-ups

- **The mechanical rule.** The list is a reconstruction by name. The client's own "Mechanical Type Check" (effects 4203 / 4204, 4217) must test something: find it in the binary and replace the list. The Straegis (`MOB_Straegis*`) are left out: their lore text says they "lack biology" but also "the characteristic signs of machine".
- **A timed disable on machines**, the grenade's 20 s disorient, once the server has a disorient or a per-effect movement-speed debuff that NPC AI reads (`MOVEMENT_SPEED_MOD`).
- **Interrupting a machine's cast or warmup** on hit, which needs a hook from the effect layer into `cimmeria-cell-combat`.
