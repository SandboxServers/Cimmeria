## The burn, effect 9110 (RECONSTRUCTION)

| Field | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `script_name` | `RangedEnergyDamage` | Existing script. It takes `FocusDamage` and `HealthDamage` from both pools each pulse, with no Focus-first gate, which is how the codebase already models energy damage. |
| `pulse_count` / `pulse_duration` | 4 / 1.0 s | One pulse on the hit, three more over three seconds. |
| `FocusDamage` / `HealthDamage` | 15 / 3 per pulse | Flame BC 2852 ("-150F -30H 8 Ticks") has a 5:1 Focus-to-Health ratio; this is a tenth of its size. A full burn, 60F / 12H, is about one extra pistol shot (641: -100F / -10H) spread over four seconds. |
| `EffectCategory` | `Burning` (nvp 9112) | Coordinator contract with AM-11c: its Antidote/Coagulant `RemoveEffects` script cleanses effects by this NVP. Required; the seed guard asserts it. The client's category is `EFFECT_Burning` (effects 2407, 3475, 3477, 3484, 4797 and 5000 remove "1 Effect of Moniker EFFECT_Burning"). |
| `ability_id` | 723 | The toggle, for provenance. The effect is not in 723's `effect_ids`, so casting 723 (which nothing does, D-AM07) would not apply it. |
| `flags` | 0 | No resist roll. |

No seeded effect was a working burn to reuse: Flame BC 2852 is seeded with `pulse_count` 1 and no script, and the six EFFECT_Burning rows only remove burns.

**Stacking** is the ADR's decision 4, through the unchanged `register_active_effect`: the same shooter's next hit refreshes the burn (the remaining pulses never shrink, and the next pulse moves a full interval out); another shooter's hit adds a burn of their own. A shooter who keeps firing faster than once a second burns the target once per hit (the hit's own pulse), and the three-pulse tail runs when they stop.

## Known limits

- **The burn ignores shields and resistances.** `RangedEnergyDamage` writes the pools directly, like every effect script (ADR decision 19), so `ABSORB_ENERGY` and energy resistance do not reduce a burn pulse. The shot itself is energy damage through `calculate_damage_penetrating`, so it does meet them. A burn honouring them needs a script in `cimmeria-cell-combat` or an absorb helper below it; not done here.
- **Mitigation is 0 in the default stats,** so penetration does nothing in live play. Incendiary's penetration is nominal (1.0) anyway, so nothing is lost. Its armour term is the energy one (`ENERGY_AF`), not the physical one, once mitigation is populated.
- **Effect 9110 is not in the client's cooked data.** Registering the burn sends `onTimerUpdate(TIMER_DURATION_EFFECT)` with effect id 9110. What the client draws for an unknown effect id (nothing, or a missing-icon buff) is unverified. This is the first ammo on-hit effect that pulses, so AM-12's UAT should look at the target's buff bar the first time an Incendiary round hits. AM-07's client push covers items, not effects.
- **The burn pulse is not scaled by the shot's modifier.** Nothing scales on-hit effects; with a 1.0 damage multiplier it makes no difference.
- **Ships dark** behind `ammo.finite_special`, like every row: no burn while the flag is off.

## Telemetry

No new events. A burning shot shows as `ammo_damage_applied` (target `ammo`, `on_hit_effect_id = 9110`), then `effect_script_dispatch` and `ranged_energy_damage` (target `abilities`) for the first pulse, `active_effect_registered` or `active_effect_refreshed` for the registration, and one `ranged_energy_damage` per later pulse.
