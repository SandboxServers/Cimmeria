Earlier `main` failures of the same job, before WC-14, were all on the

  the Postgres service container failed to initialise.

Cause of the upload failures: GitHub's results accounts
`productionresultssa9`, `16`, `17` and `18` share the Azure stamp
`bn9prdstrz04a`, whose DNS rotates `20.150.82.228`, `20.150.88.228` and
`52.239.172.36`. The colo cannot reach `52.239.172.36` (0 of 5 connects from
node1), and the node egress proxy sent only `57.0.0.0/8` through the
workstation exit, so every connect that drew that address first timed out
after 20 s: about 450 failed CONNECTs a day across node1, node2 and node5.

Fix (2026-10-11, deployed): `52.239.0.0/16` joins `57.0.0.0/8` in the node
proxies' `--via-dst` and the exit's `--allow-dst`
(`tools/ci-runners/README.md`, "Colo egress"); the upload step stays a
normal step. The exit was restarted with `start-exit.ps1`, and only the
`egress` service was rebuilt and recreated on node2, node1 and node5, so no
runner was recreated. Six CONNECTs to sa17 from node1 afterwards all went
through the exit and reached the account in about 0.5 s, including the
rotations that put `52.239.172.36` first. node3 picks the range up from
`windows/setup.ps1` on its next setup run (its pool is idle while
`CI_WINDOWS_RUNNER=hosted`).

The two Postgres service failures
are runner faults outside this campaign; if they recur in the count they go
to the runner owners, and the count records them as infrastructure, not as
test flakes (D-WC9 covers the tests).
