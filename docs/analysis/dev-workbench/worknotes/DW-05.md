# DW-05 spatial model and bookmarks

The spatial frame samples client world, player, entities, screen projections and camera through existing lab readers. A coherent sample remains `current: false` and carries `client_identity_unbound` until a trusted login generation can distinguish same-process relogs. Authored regions and spawnlist are world-filtered read-only DB rows. Server runtime entities remain unknown until DW-03S-B proves a current client/server session binding. No map asset parser or seed writer was added.

Bookmarks live in the daemon's per-user `workbench/bookmarks/<instance>.json`, use stable UUIDs, and hold BigWorld metre coordinates, facing, observation time, operator-supplied source label and optional screenshot path. Mutating routes use the browser cookie/CSRF boundary. Actions additionally require a current lab lease. Both click and teleport now fail closed with `bookmark_action_unavailable` and `session_identity_unbound` after the session, bookmark and lease checks, before any input. A known space ID remains non-actionable. Future enablement needs a trusted BoundSession checked at the actual mouse-down or final Enter boundary; an initial world check alone cannot guard a multi-step action.

Offline regression guards cover entity-ID collision across worlds/spaces/instances, stale-frame refusal, four-corner region geometry and unit annotations, bookmark persistence and update/delete, and fail-closed action refusal. Live spatial projection, native click and teleport effect are deferred to the separately approved lab UAT packet.
