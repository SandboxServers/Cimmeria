# DW-03S-H: Base-session send origins

> Status: implemented locally, pending review and integration. No live lab action.

The session send helper copies `wire_send_origin()` under the connected lock that supplied its key, sequence and ACKs. Reliable single and bundle sends carry the ticket over socket awaits. Successful delayed sends do not register old ciphertext in a replacement generation's reliable window. Unreliable witness sends use the same capture rule. Cooked-data direct replies and paced resyncs also copy the ticket with the sequence; paced resync validates its original sequence-counter token before building and before registration. The tick future captures the original origin synchronously when `run_tick_loop` is called, before Tokio schedules it, and its cancellation-token identity guards every retransmit scan. Each pending resend carries its cached ciphertext and the origin copied from the channel that yielded it; the Channel's budget and original ciphertext remain unchanged.

The only remaining production `send_to(` in the owned modules is the `send_with_origin` fallback at `helpers/mod.rs`, for a state without a Phase 3 seed. The fallback is deliberately untagged and makes proof coverage incomplete. The public retransmit helper retains legacy test callers; the tick loop uses its session-guarded variant. Because those legacy fixtures have no seed, `PendingRetransmit.origin` is optional rather than the packet's nonoptional sketch. No receiver/socket proof or complete send census is claimed; DW-03S-D/F and receive proof remain dependencies.

Regression guards: a barrier pauses A's reliable socket send while B takes the same peer, then asserts the retained A origin, byte identity and no A ciphertext in B's channel; witness unreliable and bundle emits assert the exact recipient ticket; a timed retransmit scan asserts one channel's cached bytes and A ticket survive B replacement while the old loop cannot collect B's channel; a paused tick send proves A's original ticket and no B-channel scan; direct and paced cooked-data replies both reach the origin-aware transport. Existing retransmit/tick and cooked-data suites cover byte and window behavior. No live client or visual UAT occurred.
