## Design choices

**Free Jaffa is archetype 7.** Checked against
`entities/defs/enumerations.xml` (`ARCHETYPE_Sholva` = 7,
`ARCHETYPE_Jaffa` = 8) and `crates/resources/src/base/chardef.rs` (char_defs
8 and 18, the SGU Jaffa, are archetype 7). The ledger's number is right.

**Two pickup chains, split at 5.** Conditions only AND, so "Human" and "not
Human" need a chain each. `lt 5` and `gte 5` cover every value between them:
a player with no archetype reads -1 and takes the Human branch (the engine's
standing rule for a missing archetype), and no archetype is left with a body
that does nothing. Chain 3029 is `gte 5` rather than `eq 5` for that reason: a
Goa'uld or Loyalist Jaffa visitor who was given M1559 by chain 3001 must still
be able to finish it. The Asgard gets exactly the old actions, and
`live_db_chain_3029_is_the_pre_cs05_chain_3008` pins the list.

**The tutorial grant has no `archetypes` list.** The chain gate already
selects Humans, and a list would refuse the no-archetype player the grant
while still showing them the tutorial that tells them to place Pistol Shot.

**`source_id` is the mission id** (1559 for the tutorial grant, 1569 for the
signatures), as in the vocabulary's own M687 example. CS-04 should use 622
and 687 so both starts read the same way in the provenance table.

**M1562: the SMG lies on Carter's desk (2026-10-05, owner request).** The
first build of this packet made step 4627 a second press on Carter, because
the repo holds no desk actor or position. The owner asked for the desk to be
found in the client map, so it was read with the repo's own readers
(`extract_actors`, `extract_kismet`, `tools/upk_parser.py`, `extract_map`,
`obj_slab`, `nav_inspect`); nothing was run in the game, and no client data
or tooling was added to the repo. Coordinates are BigWorld
(`(ue.y, ue.z, ue.x) / 100`), headings `atan2(dx, dz)`, evidence classes as
in the [Harset placement method](../../harset-rebuild/placements/METHOD.md).

Three things follow.

- **The lab was sealed.** Chain 3028, a port of the legacy script, plays
  10009 on arrival: that is the close. Nothing ever played 10010. A Human
  sent to Carter by the first build of this packet would have found the doors
  shut. Chain 3030 now sends 10010 three seconds after the press (the close
  is a 2.0 s clip), so the doors are open again long before the player has
  walked the corridor. A relog needs nothing: the map loads with the doors
  open and chain 3028 fires only on step 4624. For non-Humans the doors stay
  closed, as today.
- **The prop is the gun, as in the CellBlock.** M641 already has this
  pickup: spawn 11 `Preparation_SMG1A`, template 8 (the `WP_SMG_1A` mesh),
  lit with `INT_MissionWorldObject` while its step is active, chain 1055
  granting item 21 on the press. The SGC prop copies it. It uses a new
  template, 411, a clone of 8 with `interaction_type` 0: template 8 carries
  `INT_RingNetwork` (32) from the legacy data, which gives a cursor at all
  times, and a cursor with no chain behind it is a press that does nothing.
  With 0 the prop is scenery except while chain 3033 has lit it, so it cannot
  be pressed by a non-Human, before step 4627 or after the pickup.
- **Carter on the desk step.** Chain 3033 moves the marker from Carter to
  the gun when her dialog closes. If she can still be pressed on step 4627,
  chain 3036 shows dialog 5367 again, whose last line is "There's another
  weapon on my lab desk. I think you'll like that one better." It grants
  nothing. A press on Carter is therefore: step 4625, dialog 5367 and the
  step advances (3032); step 4626, dialog 5367 again (3034); step 4627,
  dialog 5367 again as the pointer (3036); any other time, and for a
  non-Human at any time, no chain (she is unmarked).

Ids taken: template 411 (the next id after 410; 200-409 and 1300-1599 are
reserved blocks, none is documented for SGC_W1) and spawn 81 (the first
unused id after SGC_W1's rows 52-77). Chain ids 3036 and 3037 are inside the
file's 3001-3050 range.

The steps are walked in order (4625 reach her, 4626 her dialog, 4627 the
desk) so the mission log reads as written. Chain 3034 answers a press during
step 4626, when the close of dialog 5367 was lost to a relog or an evicted
dialog. The markers are set by Human-only chains (3030, 3033) rather than
inside 3028, so the Asgard's elevator press is byte-for-byte what it was.

**M1569: the trigger is `mission_completed 1569`.** M1569 has one step
("Check the storage crates for any useful supplies.") and no chain; how it is
offered and how its crates behave is route content, outside v6 (OD-CS10).
`mission_completed` is the one event every possible route ends in: whichever
chain the route campaign writes will finish with `complete_mission 1569` (or
the last required `complete_objective`), and both call the same dispatcher.
`live_db_complete_mission_1569_delivers_the_rewards_once` runs that action
and gets the rewards with no other wiring. A crate chain was not used even
though a crate spawn exists (`SGC_W1_Crate`, spawnlist row 64): it would be
the route's interactable, and the mission has six objectives that suggest
more than one crate.

**M1569 items are granted directly, not through a loot table.** CellBlock's
M687 opens a loot window on its crate (`open_loot`), and CS-04 adds loot
tables 12, 13 and 14 for the same item sets (table 10 is the Commando set).
`open_loot` needs a live container to open: it takes it from the interact
trigger's target or the player's interaction pin and checks range. A
`mission_completed` event has neither, and the only container M1569 could use
is the route's crate, which is not authored. So chains 3041-3044 use
`add_item`, and this packet defines no loot table or loot id and does not
depend on CS-04's. The cost is the bag-full case (open question 5): a loot
window keeps what does not fit, a direct grant does not. First-press feedback
is the same either way once something completes the mission: the items
appear and the signature's "You have learned ..." line is sent. If the route
campaign gives 1569 a crate chain, it can open tables 10, 12, 13 and 14 from
that chain and delete the `add_item` rows here, leaving the signature grants
on `mission_completed`.

**M1569 items use container 0**, the item's own default container, which is
where a loot pickup puts them (weapons to the bandolier, armor to the
backpack, the Hologram Emitter to the mission bag). M1559 and M1562 keep
`container: 1` (backpack) for the pistol and the SMG, as chains 1005 and 1055
do in the CellBlock, so the player equips by hand.

**Guns arrive empty.** `add_item` sets 0 rounds (OD-CS13, guarded in CS-01b);
no chain loads a clip.

## Open questions

1. **M1569 has no way to complete on a live server.** No chain accepts or
   completes it, and the GM method `gmMissionComplete` (cell method 118) is
   not wired, so CS-08 cannot bring the four `SGU_* / M1569` Gear rows or the
   SGU signatures to PASS by play. Options: wire `gmMissionComplete` to the
   same path as the `complete_mission` action (a small GM-only change, no
   client patch), or record those rows as built and test-covered with UAT
   deferred to the route campaign.
2. **Which desk is Carter's** is inferred (see the table above): the map
   names none. If the owner knows it is one of the other two peninsulas
   (centres about z 30.4 and z 43.0, same x and height), spawn 81's z is the
   only number to change; `lab_placement.rs` pins the footprint and needs the
   same edit.
3. **M1562's lab fight is seeded but not wired.** Dialog 5366 has Carter
   calling for help against two Jaffa, and they exist: spawnlist rows 71 and
   77 (template 35 "SGC Jaffa", hostile, untagged) in the corridor east of
   the lab doors. Her dialog 5367 opens with "Thanks for your help.", which
   reads as if the doors opened when they died. That is not built: objective
   5365 (the kill) is optional in the seed, so the doors re-open on a timer
   instead, and chain 3032's `advance_step` closes step 4625 with objectives
   5365 and 6036 ("Enter Col. Carter's lab.") ticked whether or not the fight
   happened. Wiring the doors to the two deaths needs tags on rows 71 and 77
   and a kill-count chain, and makes the optional objective a gate: an owner
   call. An earlier version of this note said the Jaffa were not seeded; that
   was wrong.
4. **6843 Hologram Emitter** lists only container 2 (the mission bag) in
   `items.container_sets`, so the Archaeologist's M1569 emitter lands there.
   The ledger names 6843; whether a mission-bag item is the intended reward
   is worth a look in UAT.
5. **A full carried container sends a content grant to mail.** The base
   attempts normal inventory placement first. An explicit `container_full`
   refusal now mints the item into persistent system-mail escrow and tells the
   player where to collect it. This covers the M1559 pistol, the M1562 SMG and
   the M1569 items, including a full bandolier. The live-DB guard
   `live_db_full_bag_content_grant_is_escrowed_in_mail` checks the fallback.
   A failure to write the mail is logged at ERROR and shown to the player so
   a GM can recover it; a commit with an unknown outcome is never mailed,
   because that could duplicate an already committed inventory item.
6. **Visitors other than Free Jaffa** (Goa'uld, Loyalist Jaffa) still get
   M1559 from chain 3001, as before. OD-CS09 excludes Free Jaffa only.
7. **Merge order: not ahead of CS-02 (#1273).** On `main` today char_defs 8
   and 18 (Free Jaffa) still start in SGC_W1. With `archetype neq 7` on chain
   3001 a new Free Jaffa would load there with no mission, no marker on
   Hammond and nothing to press. CS-02 moves that start; this packet must
   merge after it (or with CS-06, which gives the Dakara start its content).
8. **The M1569 reward chains are not one-shot.** `missions.num_repeats` is 1
   for 1569 and the offer guard (`cell/missions/lifecycle.rs`,
   `accept_mission`) refuses a completed mission only when
   `repeats > num_repeats`, so one re-accept after completion is allowed and a
   second completion would grant the items again (the signature grant is
   idempotent). No existing condition makes a `mission_completed` chain
   once-per-character: counters are in memory and `tutorial_shown` accepts
   tutorial dialogs only. The seed comment above chains 3041-3044 and
   mission-chains.md therefore bind the route campaign: its offer chain must
   gate on `mission_status 1569 eq not_active`. If the owner wants the guard
   in the reward chains themselves, it needs a persisted per-character
   content flag, which is a new mechanism.
9. The "Seed rows" counts in the vocabulary's action table (dated
   2026-07-25) were not touched: CS-04 changes the same rows in parallel.
