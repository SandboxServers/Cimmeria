# NPC AI Restoration Audit

> Type: explanation. Audience: the coordinator and packet workers for this campaign.
> Updated: 2026-09-24. Companions: [launch prompt and decisions](README.md), [work packets](work-packets.md), [telemetry plan](telemetry.md), [evidence passes](evidence/), [2026-09-18 playtest report](../playtests/2026-09-18-colo-castle/README.md).

## Scope and evidence boundary

The owner reported four NPC behaviour problems from colo play in Castle_CellBlock (world 12) on a GM account, in normal play with no ghost or noclip:

1. Every mob after the first one outside the stasis room ignores players until it is attacked.
2. NPCs do not seek cover, and NPCs spawned in cover do not stay in it.
3. NPCs leave the ground while moving and climb into the air, especially when the player is above them on a ramp that leads down toward them.
4. Some NPCs move only so far from their spawn and then get stuck. They either stand frozen and keep attacking, or run in place. They stay stuck even when the player returns to a spot they could reach before.

Four read-only evidence passes ran on 2026-09-24 against `main` @ `192d4216` (the tree under `crates/` and `db/` is identical to `b0b594e9`):

- a state-machine and aggro audit (`npc-ai-spawn-advisor`);
- a grounding and movement audit (`movement-teleport-advisor`, including Ghidra on `SGW.exe` and the vendored Detour source);
- a cover archaeology pass (`game-archaeology-specialist`, including Ghidra);
- a SigNoz behavioural mining pass over the last 7 days of colo logs.

The full reports are in [evidence/](evidence/). This ledger reconciles them, and where they disagree it says which one wins and why. Nothing was built, run or changed in a client.

SigNoz window: the last play that reached SigNoz was 2026-09-21 16:52 UTC. About 95 world entries between 09-18 and 09-21 cover every symptom except floating, which the telemetry can only show indirectly (see T3).

Confidence tags: **[C]** confirmed in code; **[T]** confirmed in telemetry as well; **[B]** confirmed in the client binary; **[H]** high confidence, inferred; **[M]** medium; **[L]** low.

The owner warned that the existing docs have not been reliable for NPC AI. Four doc claims this campaign relied on turned out to be wrong; they are listed in [section 6](#6-documentation-that-is-wrong).

## 5. Telemetry gaps
