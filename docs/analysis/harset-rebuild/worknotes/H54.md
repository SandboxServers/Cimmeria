## The defect

`abandonMission` is a client-callable cell method and
`missions::abandon_mission` fired nothing into the content engine. Abandoning
flips a mission back to not-active with the player already past every edge, so
two things break at once:

1. **The offer gate reopens and nothing repaints it.** Every Harset offer
   chain paints its icon on `player_loaded`, which is an edge. After an
   abandon the gate is satisfied again and there is no edge left, so the offer
   does not come back until the player crosses a world boundary.
2. **The live bind is stranded.** Abandon 1324 on step 3953 and Ba'al keeps
   dialog-set 5151: he shows a stale marker and the click replays the council
   dialog, because chain 6304's step gate no longer matches.

Both self-heal on the next world transition — which rebuilds
`available_interactions` empty and re-fires every `player_loaded` chain — and
that is why nobody reported it. It is the third form of playtest finding H9,
after `enter_region` (H52) and `player_loaded` (closed in seed).

## Every abandon path

Four entry points, three handlers, all now wired. The packet said not to
assume there were exactly three, so here is the enumeration and how it was
arrived at: `grep` for `abandon_mission` / `AbandonMission` / `abandonMission`
across `crates/`, then a check of `remove_mission`, which has exactly one
non-test caller.

| Path | Entry point | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Client cell method | `cell_methods::missionary::dispatch`, index 52 | fires after a successful removal; `engine` threaded in through `dispatch::router` |
| Chain action | `content::executor::mission::abandon` (`Action::AbandonMission`) | fires after a successful removal; `player_id` and `engine` threaded in from `execute_one` |
| `gmMissionClear` (110) | `cell_methods::gm::missions::handle_mission_clear` | one handler, fires after a successful removal |
| `gmMissionAbandon` (120) | the same handler | both indices route there; the test drives both |

Two things are **not** abandon paths, checked and ruled out: the console
`.missionfail` command (`cell::console::mission`) sets `MISSION_FAILED` and
never removes the instance, and `cell::missions::persist` only serialises.

`abandon_mission` now returns whether it actually removed an instance, and
every hook is gated on that. A client can send `abandonMission` for any id;
without the gate, every stray call would repaint every seeded offer.

## Ordering is the contract

The dispatcher populates world, archetype and mission context **after**
`abandon_mission` removes the instance. That is what lets a repaint chain
carry the offer chain's own `mission_status <id> eq not_active` gate verbatim
— the single most important property of the whole packet, because every
repaint chain in the seed does exactly that. Populate before, and all three
seed chains fail closed and the packet silently does nothing.

`the_context_reflects_the_post_removal_state` is the guard: a chain gated on
the mission still being `active` must NOT fire. It is the mirror image of the
positive, and it fails when the fire is moved ahead of the removal.

The dispatcher is also in `event_dispatch/world_context_contract_tests.rs`,
which exists to catch a dispatcher that forgets `populate_world_context` — a
shape merges have produced three times already.

## Seed

Three chains, all gated on world 68 (`Harset_CmdCenter`), all unbinding before
they rebind.

| Chain | Mission | File | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6308 | 1324 Present Yourself | `harset_jaffa_chains.sql` | clear 5151@42 (Ba'al's council bind) and 120001@54 (Moh'katan's turn-in), then bind 5149@54 (the offer) |
| 6342 | 1326 Lan'toc | `harset_jaffa_chains.sql` | clear 5161@54 (the turn-in), then bind 5159@54 (the offer) |
| 6120 | 742 Giving the Walls Ears | `harset_goauld_chains.sql` | clear 3130@43 (Anat's in-progress topic), then bind 3127@43 (the offer) |

Each carries its offer chain's condition set **verbatim**, including the
prerequisites: 6342 keeps 6331's `1324 completed` / `1325 completed`, and 6120
keeps 6118's `1200 completed`. Dropping a prerequisite would repaint an offer
the player cannot accept, and the accept chain's own gate would then refuse
the click — a permanently dead icon, which is worse than no icon.

Three authoring rules came out of writing them, all now in the seed comments
and in `content-engine.md`:

- **Unbind before rebind.** `interactions/dispatch/interact.rs` takes the
  first bound entry on a template that has a dialog, so an `add_dialog_set`
  ordered before the matching `remove_dialog_set` leaves the NPC handing out
  the old dialog. `every_abandon_chain_unbinds_before_it_rebinds` is driven
  over all three chains so a fourth is covered without editing the test.
- **Clear every candidate bind, do not choose.** The step is gone by the time
  the chain runs, so 6308 cannot tell 3953 from 3954. `remove_dialog_set` on
  an empty slot is a safe no-op.
- **Gate on the world where the stale state is observable.** An abandon from
  anywhere else needs nothing.

Chain id 6308 was taken from the block comment that had parked 6308-6310 for a
future arrival-dialog chain; that note now reads 6309-6310 and says why. The
arrival dialog is faction-neutral and was only ever parked in 1324's range.

### Offer chains examined and deliberately given no abandon chain

| Mission | Why not |
|---|---|
| 1200 Meet Your Queen | Accepted on arrival by chain 6121 (`player_loaded Harset`), not from an offer. An abandon chain that re-accepted would make 1200 unabandonable the moment a player drops it in world 57 — a behaviour change this packet was not asked to make. Its in-progress binds do strand on an abandon in world 68, but they self-heal on the next world transition and there is no offer state to repaint them into. **Coordinator decision needed.** |
| 1361 Meet The Praxis | Its acceptance chains 6511-6513 ship disabled pending M0 and are pinned by `praxis_acceptance_is_enabled_iff_the_return_door_is`. Whoever flips 6511-6513 at M0 should author the twin in the same change; the seed comment says so and names 6308 as the model. |
| 1360 Frost's Letter | Its offer is not a chain in this file — the letter is granted upstream — so an abandon reopens no gate and leaves no offer to repaint. Its only bind (5356 on Marsh) is `step_status`-gated and dies with the mission. |

The path tests drive the *entry points* — `missionary::dispatch`,
`executor::execute_actions`, `gm::dispatch` — rather than the dispatcher, so
deleting a hook fails them; a direct call would not. The replay guards set
`mission_id` in the context, without which every negative would pass
vacuously.

Revert proofs, all run:

| Revert | Fails |
|---|---|
| hook removed from `missionary::dispatch` | 1 |
| hook removed from `executor::mission::abandon` | 1 |
| hook removed from `gm::missions::handle_mission_clear` | 1 |

| seed: chain 6308's `add_dialog_set` reordered ahead of its unbinds | 2 |

loaded test database rather than by editing the `.sql` and reloading, so both
