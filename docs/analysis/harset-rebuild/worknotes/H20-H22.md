## Deviations from the packet text

**H22 asked for `complete_objective 4551` on both branches; both branches use
`advance_step` instead.** 4551 is step 3960's only non-optional objective
(`mission_objectives.sql`), and `cell/missions/progression.rs::complete_objective`
calls `mission.complete()` as soon as every non-optional objective of the
current step is done — so `complete_objective 4551` completes the whole of
1326 and orphans the turn-in step 4603. `advance_step` force-completes 4551
on its way, so the objective is still completed exactly once and the client
still gets its tick. The same reasoning retires `complete_objective` from
1324. A file-wide structural guard in `mission_1324.rs` asserts no chain in
6301-6500 uses the verb at all, so H21 and H23-H28 inherit it.

**The H20 arrival dialog 6169 is documented, not authored.** Both delivery
paths are dead against today's data: `display_dialog` on a `player_loaded`
trigger has no entity to resolve the client's portrait lookup against (no
`target_entity_id`, no `last_interaction_target` pin, and 6169 is not a
monologue — 3 of its 5 screens speak as 3219), and `add_dialog_set 7016`
needs a `slot`, which is an `entity_templates.template_id`, and no template
anywhere references speaker 3219. The seed file carries the exact chain 6308
that lands the moment M0/H14 produces an arrival NPC template. Precedent for
shipping the hole rather than a silent no-op is H10's chain 6007. The OP-CORE
and Goa'uld lanes are still told not to author 6169.

**1326 is unreachable until H21 lands 1325.** Both offer chains carry
`mission_status 1325 eq completed`. Gating on 1324 alone would make 1325's
and 1326's offers collide on Moh'katan the moment H21 ships, and two offer
chains matching one right-click both push a `display_dialog` — the player
sees the second and the first dialog's button is rejected by the #479 gate.

**Fidelity note, recorded not fixed:** the refusing Jaffa is not removed from
the city. "Then you must leave the city" (dialog 4376 screen 80992) is text
only; D-H03 forbids `destroy_entity` / `set_visible` on a shared-hub NPC and
there is no per-witness visibility overlay, so hiding him for one player
hides him for everyone. Unkeepable by construction while the hub is shared.

**Fidelity note, recorded not fixed:** "More Info" is indistinguishable from
"Accept" on dialogs 4357 and 4373. Both screens carry button 9 "More Info"
and button 8 "Accept" (`dialog_screen_buttons.sql`), `fire_dialog_choice`
puts `button_id` in the context but no authorable condition reads it, and
`handle_dialog_button_choice` clears `open_dialog_id` before firing. So a
player who clicks More Info has already accepted, and their subsequent Accept
click is rejected by the #479 gate and appears to do nothing. Net outcome is
benign; "nothing happens when I click Accept" is what a UAT will report.
Recovering the real two-button behaviour needs a `button_id` condition in the
loader.

**Open question for UAT:** dialog 4374's last screen (118960) carries an
Accept button that nothing consumes. 4374 is the only one of the nine H20/H22
dialogs with a button on a non-blurb screen, which reads as evidence the 2009
accept for 1326 sat on `dialog_choice '4374'` with 4373's More Info opening
4374 first. Not restructured on that reading without UAT — moving the accept
to 4374 would strand any player who clicks Accept on the blurb.

## Playtest lessons applied, and what changed because of them

Four of the six findings in the
[2026-09-18 Castle report](../../playtests/2026-09-18-colo-castle/README.md)
bear on this lane. Two changed the seed.

### Edge-trigger race (finding H9) — added chain 6341

Chain 6331 paints 1326's offer on `player_loaded 'Harset_CmdCenter'`, which
is an **edge**: it runs on login, gate travel and the cross-world hop, and
never again while the player stands still. But 1326's gate flips to satisfied
the instant 1325 completes, and **1325 is turned in to Moh'katan, in world
68** — the player is already standing in the Command Center when the
condition becomes true. With 6331 alone the "?" would not have appeared until
they walked out and back in, which reads to a player as the next mission
simply not existing. Exactly the Castle shape: a chain keyed on an edge and
gated on a step that activates while the player is already inside.

New chain 6341 closes it on `mission_completed '1325'`, which is a level
trigger. `fire_mission_completed` runs after `complete_mission_direct` has
flipped the status and populates world, archetype and the full mission
context, so 6341 carries 6331's identical condition set and is evaluated
against the post-completion state. The two are separate chain ids rather than
one chain with two trigger rows, because `load_single_chain_for_test` returns
only the first expansion and a test written the obvious way would then guard
one trigger and silently ignore the other.

**The coordinator must pass this to H21.** When 1324 completes — chain 6305,
also in world 68 — 1325's offer needs the same `mission_completed '1324'`
partner or 1325 inherits precisely this bug. Chain 6305's action order
(remove-before-complete) already reserves the slot for it.

The rest of the lane was audited for the same shape and is clean: 6334 and
6339 both paint states that are entered in the *other* world, so a
cross-world hop is unavoidable and `player_loaded` always follows.

### Two copies of one identity (the Zuritska finding) — flipped dsm 120002

See [the next section](#the-dsm-120002-flip). One bind lights both Lan'toc
Jaffa, which is intended, but the WIP's version of the row would have let the
Jaffa scripted to *refuse* open the oath dialog.

## The dsm 120002 flip

The WIP put dialog 4375 (the oath) on dsm 120002, on the stated reasoning
that a NULL `dialog_id` was dropped at load and would make the bind a silent
cache miss. That **was** true and stopped being true with Castle packet CA02,
which arrived in this lane with the wave2 merge:
`cell/spawner/dialogs.rs::load_dialog_set_maps` now keeps such rows as
`DialogSetMapEntry { dialog_id: None, .. }` precisely so a bind can raise an
indicator with no dialog behind it.

Naming a dialog was also the latent bug the WIP's own comment flagged. Both
Lan'toc Jaffa are template 204 and `send_interaction_update_if_visible` fans
the merged flags to every template-204 entity in the player's AoI, so one
bind lights both — that part is intended, either Jaffa satisfies the step.
But the click routes per NPC, and `handle_interact` opens the first bound
entry that *has* a dialog. In any state where chains 6335/6336 stop matching,
a dialog on this row opens on **both** NPCs and the Jaffa scripted to refuse
swears allegiance instead. With `dialog_id` NULL, `find_map` steps over the
row and that path opens nothing: a dead click instead of the wrong outcome.

The replay guard asserts `dialog_id IS NULL`, so it is also the tripwire if
CA02's loader behaviour is ever reverted.

**Constraint this creates for H14 / M0:** template 204 must carry *only*
these two mission Jaffa in world 57. An ambient Jaffa reusing slot 204 would
light up with a mission cue and then do nothing when clicked. Templates 205
(`Harset_SuspiciousJaffa`) and 206 (`Harset_AngryJaffa`) are the identical
body set and are what ambient Ra's-Jaffa population should use.

| 4 | The seed file was written LF; every other file under `db/resources/Content/Seed/` is CRLF | Whole-file diff on the next Windows edit |

Two verified-false claims in the WIP's comments were also corrected: dialog
4363 has **10** screens (81723-81732), not 17; and Route B opens the first
bound entry that *has* a dialog (`entries.iter().find_map(...)`), not
`entries.first()`.

A fifth thing was documented rather than changed: **dialogs 4375 and 4376
have no buttons at all, and chains 6337/6338 depend on that.** A dialog with
zero `dialog_screen_buttons` rows sends `Event_NetOut_DialogButtonChoice`
with `ButtonId = -1` when the player closes it; a dialog that *has* buttons
sends nothing on close and fires only on a click. So keying `dialog_choice`
on a button-less dialog is correct and precedented (`Castle.py` did it for
2574/2575; shipped chains 1020/1021 do it for 2300/5020) — and adding a
button to either one silently strands the mission on step 3960 with no error
on any layer. A DB-level guard
(`the_lantoc_outcome_dialogs_stay_button_less`) now pins it, because the
break is in the client's send decision and no chain-replay context can model
it.

### Revert verification

The acceptance rule is that a test which passes with the new seed rows
deleted is not a guard. Proven by deleting them from the loaded database in
place and re-running:

```sql
DELETE FROM resources.content_actions    WHERE chain_id BETWEEN 6301 AND 6345;
DELETE FROM resources.content_conditions WHERE chain_id BETWEEN 6301 AND 6345;
DELETE FROM resources.content_triggers   WHERE chain_id BETWEEN 6301 AND 6345;
DELETE FROM resources.content_chains     WHERE chain_id BETWEEN 6301 AND 6345;
DELETE FROM resources.dialog_set_maps    WHERE dialog_set_map_id IN (120001, 120002);
```

| Run | Result |
|---|---|
| Seed rows removed | 45 tests run: 28 passed, **17 failed** — 7 in `mission_1324`, 10 in `mission_1326` |
| Reloaded from `db/database.sql` | 52 passed, 0 failed |

Both missions therefore have far more than the required one failing guard.
Three tests correctly kept passing with the rows gone, and each is a
"must not exist" or shipped-data assertion rather than a seed guard:
`no_harset_jaffa_chain_completes_an_objective`,
`no_harset_jaffa_chain_sets_a_global_interaction_bit` and
`the_lantoc_outcome_dialogs_stay_button_less`. The ordering guard
`every_remove_dialog_set_precedes_its_chains_complete_mission` carries an
explicit anti-vacuity assertion (`!rows.is_empty()`, "this guard has lost its
subject") and failed on it, which is the behaviour intended.
