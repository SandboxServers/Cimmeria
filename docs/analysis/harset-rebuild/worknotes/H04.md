## 1. What shipped

A new content-engine trigger, `entity_health_below`, authored as a
`resources.content_triggers` row with `event_type = 'entity_health_below'`
and `event_key = "<tag>:<pct>"` (e.g. `"Rinla_Malac:30"`). It fires once
per **downward crossing** of that percentage on a tagged entity, with the
attacking player as the acting player. No new action verb: the ritual end
stays `set_npc_ai_state submit` + `advance_step`, both of which already
exist.

### Crossing detection is stateless — and why

The dispatch site does not know which thresholds content has seeded, and
keeps no per-entity bookkeeping. It emits **one event per damaging hit**
carrying the target's health percentage before and after the hit
(`pct_before`, `pct_after`), and `Trigger::matches` does the band test:

```text
fires iff  entity_tag matches  &&  pct_before > pct  &&  pct_after <= pct
```

Consequences, all intended and all pinned by tests:

| Situation | Result | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 60% → 40% against a `:50` chain | fires once | the crossing hit |
| 40% → 25% against the same chain | does not fire | `pct_before > 50` is false |
| healed to 80%, then 80% → 45% | fires again | a genuine second crossing |
| `:50` and `:30` on one tag, hit 60% → 40% | only `:50` fires | independent bands |
| `:50` and `:30` on one tag, hit 80% → 20% | both fire | one hit spans both |
| hit that lands exactly on the threshold | fires | "at or below" |
| hit that **kills** | fires `entity_dead_tag`, not this | see below |

The alternative considered and rejected was firing one event per integer
percent crossed (up to 100 `resolve_event` calls on a single big hit, on
every hit in the game). The band form costs one event and one hash lookup
per hit, and the hash lookup short-circuits to nothing when no chain is
registered for the trigger type.

### The killing-blow contract is enforced at the dispatcher, not the predicate

The band predicate alone would match a `31% → 0%` hit against a `:30`
chain. The suppression lives in `fire_health_below_for_hit`, which drops
the event when the target is dead after the hit. Deadness is read from
`BSF_DEAD`, **not** from `health.cur <= 0`: an effect script runs after
the NVP damage path and outside its `target_died` guard, so a heal script
on a killing blow can leave a corpse at positive health (combat-advisor
finding, §5 below). A health-based check would then fire a threshold
chain on that corpse.

### Ownership deviation to flag

The packet lists the damage-apply hook as being in
`crates/services/src/cell/combat/`. It is not: `mark_npc_dead` is
*defined* in `cell/combat/state.rs` but *reached from*
`cell/abilities/damage_apply/mod.rs`, and the only layer with a
`&ChainEngine` in scope is `cell/abilities/use_ability/kill_credit.rs`.
The hook therefore lands in `kill_credit.rs`, outside the paths the packet
named. The diff there is 14 lines (a snapshot widened from a bool to a
`(bool, Option<HealthPct>)` tuple, and one call in the existing
`if !just_died` early-return). The pure percentage arithmetic *is* in
`cell/combat/` as the packet intended.

## 3. The hook

`handle_use_ability_with_kill_credit` already snapshots
`was_alive_before` and re-checks after the ability resolves; the health
percentage is snapshotted in the same pass, because once
`handle_use_ability` returns the pre-hit value is gone. The existing
`just_died` branch is untouched; the new call sits in the `!just_died`
early-return, so the two events are mutually exclusive per hit by
construction.

Five production call sites reach this wrapper (confirmed by the combat
advisor): `cell_methods/player/combat/mod.rs:54`,
`cell_methods/player/world/auto_cycle.rs:114`,
`cell_methods/player/interaction/interact.rs:135`,
`service/ticks/pending_holster.rs:72`, `service/ticks/auto_cycle.rs:185`.
**No player single-target attack path bypasses it.**

## 4. Behaviour implemented vs gaps

Implemented: the trigger, the loader arm, the matcher, the dispatcher,
the single-target player damage hook, and the doc row text for H99.

Known gaps, each deliberate and none blocking H21:

1. **AoE secondaries do not fire threshold crossings.** Cone secondaries
   and ground-AoE secondaries reach `apply_damage_to_target` directly and
   only surface *deaths* to the caller layer. The combat advisor confirmed
   cone secondaries explicitly exclude the primary target
   (`cone_aoe/geometry.rs:74`), and ground AoE routes its primary through
   `handle_use_ability` — so a duel target taking the hit as the primary is
   always visible to the hook. The loss is a *bystander* NPC crossing a
   threshold as an AoE secondary. Closing it needs an attacker-side
   scratchpad in `crates/entity` mirroring `last_aoe_deaths`; out of scope
   here.
2. **Damage-over-time pulses do not fire it.** `cell/effects/pulsing/tick.rs`
   mutates NPC health with no `ChainEngine` in scope. This is a **content
   constraint for H21**: a DoT on Mala'c that crosses 30% is invisible, and
   because `pct_before` is then already ≤30 on every subsequent hit, the
   trigger never fires — hard softlock. Do not put a DoT on the duel NPC.
3. **The `apply_effect` content action** (`content/effect_apply.rs:170`,
   PR #619) can damage without firing the trigger. Content-authored, so
   acceptable and documented rather than plumbed.
4. **No non-lethal damage cap** (explicitly excluded by the packet). See
   the softlock note in §5.

## 7. Integration requests and hand-offs

**A — mission 1325 needs a death-path fallback chain (H21, seed).** Two
independent routes end the duel with the NPC dead and the step never
advanced: the 2× player-damage hack means one hit from ~35% can go from
above 30% to dead, and a groupmate landing the crossing hit evaluates the
step gate on *their* mission context, drops the chain, and then keeps
shooting. Ship an `entity_dead_tag` chain on the duel NPC's tag, gated on
the same `step_status 1325 / 3959 eq active`, that also advances to 4036.
One seed row converts a hard softlock into a slightly-wrong cutscene.
Instance/owner-gating the duel NPC is the better fix if H03's spawner
supports it — ship the fallback row either way.

**B — `npc_ai_submit` leaks player combat state (owner: whoever owns
`cell/service/npc_ai/`).** `npc_ai_submit` clears the NPC's `threat_list`
directly instead of routing through
`combat::threat::clear_dead_npc_from_all_player_threat`, so every player
who aggroed the NPC keeps it in `threatened_mobs` forever: `BSF_InCombat`
stuck set, weapon stays drawn, and `regen_tick` (gated on
`threatened_mobs.is_empty()`) never regenerates that player again. Same
failure class as the respawn `threatened_mobs.clear()` fix. Fix is ~3
lines: call `clear_dead_npc_from_all_player_threat` *before* clearing
`threat_list`, then `send_entity_method(ON_STATE_FIELD_UPDATE)` for each
returned `(player_id, new_state)` pair. **H04 is the first feature that
reliably reaches `Submit` from combat**, so this stops being latent the
moment H04 + H21 ship together. Not fixed here because
`cell/service/npc_ai/lifecycle.rs` is outside H04's owned paths in a
seven-packet parallel wave.

**C — Submit does not disengage the attacker (same owner as B, or H21).**
`set_npc_ai_state submit` changes `ai_state` only — not `faction`, not
`interaction_type_flags` — and does not clear the attacker's auto-cycle.
`auto_cycle_tick` re-fires every cooldown and kills the surrendered NPC
seconds later, which reads to a player as "the trigger didn't work".
Needs `combat::clear_auto_cycle` on the attacker plus a broadcast, or
flipping the NPC off `HOSTILE_FACTION`, in the submit handler or in the
chain.

**D2 — `crates/services/logs/` is not gitignored (coordinator, repo-wide).**
Running the services test suite writes `seed-authoring-*.sql` artifacts

**D — H99 doc row.** For the trigger table in

```markdown
| `OnEntityHealthBelow { entity_tag, pct }` | A tagged entity's health crossed *downward* through `pct` percent of max on a single hit. Stateless: matches only when `pct_before > pct` and `pct_after <= pct`, so one fire per crossing with no per-entity bookkeeping, and a re-fire if the entity is healed back above and crosses again. Two thresholds on one tag each fire on their own crossing, and a single large hit spanning both fires both. Seed `event_key` convention is `"<tag>:<pct>"` (e.g. `"Rinla_Malac:30"`), parsed right-to-left so a tag containing `:` still resolves — note the numeric field is *last*, the reverse of `player_in_cover_duration`. An empty tag or a `pct` outside `1..=100` rejects the chain. A killing blow fires `entity_dead_tag` instead and never fires this — that exclusion is enforced by the dispatcher (`fire_health_below_for_hit`), not by the predicate. Fired only from the single-target player damage path: AoE secondaries, DoT pulses and `apply_effect` damage do not raise it |
```
