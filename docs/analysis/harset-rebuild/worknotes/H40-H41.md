## Playtest lessons applied, and what changed because of them

### Two copies of one named NPC (Castle's two Zuritskas)

Petbe has a talk template (163, faction 1, spawn 223) and a hostile twin
(221, faction 10, `Petbe (hostile)`, staff weapon) used by mission 1245
as `Storage_Petbe`. Nothing in this lane touches 221: the marker on Petbe
rides the dsm 3129 binding on template 163, and 1245 is a different
packet in a different world. **Changed:** added
`no_goauld_chain_addresses_the_hostile_petbe_template`, a live-DB scan of
every `content_actions` row in 6101-6300 asserting none binds, spawns or
tags template 221 or `Storage_Petbe`. The guard covers the whole Goa'uld
range, so H42-H47 inherit it.

### Edge-trigger race (finding H9), REAL and fixed

**This was a live defect in the WIP.** Chain 6118 bound Anat's 742 offer
on `player_loaded Harset_CmdCenter`, gated `mission_status 1200 eq
completed`. Mission 1200 completes at Anat (chain 6123, dialog 5435), and
Anat is spawn 222 in **world 68, the Command Center**. So at the instant
the gate opened, the player had already crossed the only boundary that
fires the chain. The offer would not have appeared until they walked out
of the Command Center and back in, with nothing telling them to.

**Changed:** chain 6118 now carries a second trigger row,
`mission_completed '1200'`. `fire_mission_completed`
(`event_dispatch/mission.rs`) calls `populate_world_context` (which sets
`world_id`), `populate_mission_context` and stamps `archetype`, and runs
*after* the instance flips to MISSION_COMPLETED, so all four existing
conditions evaluate correctly with no change to the condition rows.

No other chain in the lane has this shape: 6125 (Royal Guard) is reached
by travelling from world 57 to 68, which fires `player_loaded`; 6121
(arrival accept) fires on any entry to world 57 including a return
through the Command Center door.

### Dialog displacement (two dialogs within about 3 s)

The lane contains exactly **one** `display_dialog` (2637, on chain 6103),
and it is the only chain keyed on `item_use 2819`. The D-H20 offer chains
show no dialog of their own: 6118 binds a topic, and 6119 fires on the
player having already read 2636. The 1200 arrival accept (6121) shows
nothing. **No collision exists and nothing was changed.** Note for the
other faction lanes: 6121 fires on `player_loaded Harset`, so a Jaffa or
OP-CORE lane that adds a `display_dialog` on the same event for the same
player would collide. The archetype gates keep them disjoint today.

### `set_interaction_type` is zone-wide on the bug baskets, REAL and fixed (H41-B2)

**This was the second live defect.** Chains 6104-6106 each ended with
`set_interaction_type ~ INT_MissionWorldObject` on their own basket tag.

- `set_interaction_type` mutates the shared
  `CellEntity.interaction_type_flags` and broadcasts to every witness
  (`executor/world/mod.rs`). World 57 Harset is a shared hub, not an
  instance.
- Template 164 (`Merchant Basket (Giving The Walls Ears)`) ships
  `interaction_type = 0` in `entity_templates.sql`. The mission-set bit
  is the **only** thing that makes a basket clickable.

Composition: player A plants in FirstBug, the bit clears for every player
in world 57, and player B, also on step 2504 with objective 2913 still
active, now sees scenery. B's client never sends the click, so B's own
`objective_status` gate never gets evaluated. B is stuck until a relog,
and under H41-B1 the relog repaint does not work either.

**Changed:** the three clears are removed. "Set, never clear" for props in
a shared world. What is lost is cosmetic, a planted basket keeps glowing
for the player who planted it, and the mission log still ticks. A second
click on an already-planted basket resolves nothing, because the
per-player guard was always the `objective_status` condition, never the
bit. The reversion guard is in
`each_basket_completes_its_own_objective_and_consumes_one_scarab`.

## The optional objective 5399 (explicitly checked)

Objective 5399 is `is_hidden = t, is_optional = t` on step 3585, verified
in `mission_objectives.sql:473`. It cannot block and cannot prematurely
complete 1200:

- `all_required_complete` filters optional objectives out
  (`progression.rs:176-180`), so chain 6124's `complete_objective 5399`
  leaves the required 4140 active and the mission running. Asserted by
  `chain_6124_completes_only_the_optional_objective`.
- Chain 6123 carries **no** condition on 5399 at all, so a player who
  skipped Ba'al finishes exactly as one who did. Asserted across all
  three of 5399's statuses by `the_hidden_optional_never_blocks_completion`.
- Chain 6123 retires Ba'al's topic whether or not it was taken, so no
  dangling marker is left on him.
- Step 3584 advances with `advance_step`, never `complete_objective`:
  4139 is its only required objective and completing it would end 1200
  before the player ever reached Anat.

**One residual risk for H50, not introduced here.** Hydration hardcodes
`optional: false` (`player_init/mod.rs:172-188`), so a restored optional
objective would count toward `all_required_complete`. It does not bite
1200 today only because hydration also loses the objective ids entirely
(H41-B1): 5399 is simply absent after a relog rather than present and
mis-flagged. **When H50 restores the objective arrays it must carry
`hidden` and `optional` through, or it will turn a latent bug into a live
one. A relogged player on step 3585 who then talks to Ba'al would
complete 1200 early.** This is the single most important item in this
note for the H50 owner.

- `SecondBug` and `ThirdBug` (template 164) have **no `spawnlist` rows**;
  only spawn 224 `FirstBug` exists. Chains 6103, 6105, 6106, 6108, 6109,
  6114 and 6115 name those tags and are inert until H14 seeds them. No

- Nerus (template 53) has no spawn row; H12 owns it. Chains 6110 and 6117
  bind by **template**, so they start working the moment the spawn lands.
- The Royal Guard (`CmdCenter_RoyalGuard`, template 209) and Ba'al
  (`CmdCenter_Baal`, template 42) likewise bind by template, not tag.

Anat (222) and Petbe (223) both carry a **NULL `tag`**, which is why every
NPC binding in this file is `add_dialog_set` (template-addressed) rather
than `set_interaction_type` (tag-addressed). A `set_interaction_type`
aimed at either could never resolve.

### On H50 (per-objective state is not persisted), H41-B1

Objective ids never reach the database, so every `objective_status`
condition evaluates FALSE after any relog, gate hop or cross-world
teleport. Affected: 6104-6109 (the baskets), 6113-6115 (their restore),
6124 and 6127 (1200's hidden optional). Everything gated only on
`step_status` is durable.

Consequences the coordinator should know:

1. **Mission 742 step 2504 is not completable across a relog** until H50
   lands. This is not worked around in seed, per packet instruction.
2. **A server restart during step 2504 leaves all three baskets dark for
   everyone**, because a restart resets `interaction_type_flags` to the
   template default (0) and the restore chains 6113-6115 cannot fire.
3. The defect is pinned, not hidden, by
   `objective_params_do_not_survive_the_production_hydration_shape` in
   `mission_742.rs`. **That test must be INVERTED when H50 lands**, and
   its failure message says so. The basket relog-restore acceptance for
   step 2504 is explicitly NOT claimed by this packet.

Grepping for `H50` across `mission_742.rs` and `mission_1200.rs` finds
every site the H50 owner has to revisit.
