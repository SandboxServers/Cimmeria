The only auction service the engine has is the Black Market auctioneer (F38, D-UO36): stock template 305 (moniker 7133, `INT_AUCTION` 4) plus an `interact_tag` chain running `open_black_market`. This packet places one more spawn of template 305 in the Auction House and copies chain 13008 (Debug Area) onto Omega's tag. No new template, no code. Without the Cimmeria client patch the window does not open; the chat line is the first-press feedback.
