**Row rules** (D-UO29; UO-25's `assert_list` enforces every one):

1. 1 to 20 rows per list; each design once per list; `quantity` 1.
2. The design is a real `resources.items` row, `flags & 1024` (CAN_BE_SOLD) is set, and it is not 21, 55 or 3437.
3. The design's `tier` equals the list's tier. No tier 5 list (D-UO28).
4. Heavy weapons 5094 (Rak'tel), 6617 (Serpent Jaffa Cannon) and 4565 (Serpent Ribbon Device) only in a list of their own tier, and only at tier 2 or higher.
5. Price: weapons `naquadah = 200 × tech_comp`; armor `naquadah = 100 × tech_comp`.
6. Exclude: names containing `Test`, `NO ITEM NAME` or `Instrument`; mini-game items; Asgard programs; any Human design (`WP-Human.` or `AR_H_`).
7. **Classification** (record the query you used and its counts). A Jaffa weapon is a Staff, Zat'ni'katel, Ribbon Device, Rak'tel or Jaffa Cannon by name (row 3849 `Boreal Staff`, `items.sql:10953`, has a NULL `visual_component`), with a non-empty `ammo_types` or a `max_melee_range` above 0. A Jaffa armor piece has `visual_component` or `icon_location` naming `AR_J_`, or a Jaffa set family by name (UO-06's tier-1 families: Standard, Jackal Melee, Falcon Ranged, Praxis Defensive).
8. `discipline_ids` appear to split Jaffa (21-27, row 3849) from Human (40-49, row 3145); state whether that holds for your picks.
9. **Selection** (deterministic). Weapons: group the tier's eligible designs by class (staff, zat'ni'katel, ribbon device, heavy). Within a class sort by `tech_comp`, then `item_id`. Take one per class in that order, round-robin, until 20 rows or the designs run out.
10. Armor: group the tier's eligible pieces by set family (the name without its slot word: Boots, Breastplate, Gauntlets, Helmet, Leggings). Sort families by their lowest `tech_comp`, then name. Take whole families (every slot at the family's lowest `tech_comp` in that tier) until the next family would pass 20 rows.
