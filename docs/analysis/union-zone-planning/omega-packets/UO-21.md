**Row rules** (D-UO29; UO-25's `assert_list` enforces every one, so a row that breaks one fails the W8 lane):

1. 1 to 20 rows per list; each design once per list; `quantity` 1.
2. The design is a real `resources.items` row, `flags & 1024` (CAN_BE_SOLD) is set, and it is not 21, 55 or 3437 (the designs sell list 2 buys back).
3. The design's `tier` equals the list's tier. No tier 5 list (D-UO28).
4. Heavy weapons 4990 (GL 21 Grenade Launcher), 6491 (GAU-2/A Minigun) and 6480 (Baby Mongoose Flamethrower) only in a list of their own tier, and only at tier 2 or higher.
5. Price: weapons `naquadah = 200 × tech_comp`; armor `naquadah = 100 × tech_comp`.
6. Exclude: names containing `Test`, `NO ITEM NAME` or `Instrument`; mini-game items; Asgard programs; any Jaffa design (rule 8).
7. **Classification** (record the query you used and its counts). A Human weapon has `visual_component` starting `WP-Human.` (row 3145, `items.sql:10545`) or is a heavy weapon from rule 4; it has a non-empty `ammo_types` or a `max_melee_range` above 0. A Human armor piece has `visual_component` starting `AR_H_` (row 6963, `items.sql:2603`).
8. Jaffa designs (Staff, Zat'ni'katel, Ribbon Device, Rak'tel, Jaffa Cannon, `AR_J_` armor) never go in a Human list. `discipline_ids` appear to split Human (40-49, row 3145) from Jaffa (21-27, row 3849); state whether that holds for your picks.
9. **Selection** (deterministic, so the reviewer can re-run it). Weapons: group the eligible designs of the tier by class (assault rifle, SMG, pistol, shotgun, LMG, rifle or sniper, melee, dart gun, heavy), read from the name and `icon_location`. Within a class sort by `tech_comp`, then `item_id`. Take one per class in the class order above, round-robin, until 20 rows or the designs run out.
10. Armor: group the tier's eligible pieces by set family (the name without its slot word: Boots, Gloves, Helmet, Pants, Vest). Sort families by their lowest `tech_comp`, then name. Take whole families (every slot at the family's lowest `tech_comp` in that tier) until the next family would pass 20 rows.
