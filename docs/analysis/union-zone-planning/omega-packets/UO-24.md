## Why

W8 and W9 run up to six coders at once on the same four seed files, two test `mod.rs` files and one linter allowlist. This packet creates every shared line once, so each later packet writes only between its own marker pair and adds no `mod` line. It also amends the one guard that would refuse the service chains: `live_db_omega_writes_no_interact_tag_chain` forbids every `interact_tag` chain in Omega's block (F4). The owner's 2026-10-19 answers (D-UO19 c, D-UO31) need `interact_tag` chains on the auctioneer, the medic, the guides and (D-UO32) the inn and battlemaster. Those NPCs carry no dialog set, so the F4 risk (one click opens two dialogs) cannot arise. The new guard proves that.

It also fixes three stale comments about F21 (they say O5645 and O5646 are both optional; S4940 also has required O5647 and O5644).
