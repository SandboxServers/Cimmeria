## Why

The owner chose every tier and every crafting specialty (D-UO8 B). **Row rules** (D-UO29; UO-25's `assert_list` enforces every one, called with tier `None` for these lists):

1. 1 to 20 rows per list; each design once per list; `quantity` 1.
2. The design is a real `resources.items` row, `flags & 1024` (CAN_BE_SOLD) is set, and it is not 21, 55 or 3437.
3. Consumables at `tier = 1`: `naquadah` 25 for a Slappack, 50 for an Antidote, 100 for a Mark III Stimpack; a tier-1 consumable of any other family is not stocked. Consumables above tier 1: `naquadah = 25 × tech_comp`.
4. Crafting: `naquadah = 25 × tech_comp`, every tier.
5. Exclude: names containing `Test`, `NO ITEM NAME` or `Instrument`; mini-game items; Asgard programs; weapons and armor.
6. **Selection** (deterministic). Consumables: first UO-06's ten tier-1 rows (designs 2893, 6657, 6659, 6656, 6677, 6678, 6679, 6680, 6681, 6682), each re-checked against rules 1-3. Then the Slappack, Antidote and Stimpack families above tier 1, sorted by `tier`, then `tech_comp`, then `item_id`, until 20 rows.
7. Crafting, per specialty: the eligible designs are the names ending `(<Specialty>)` or `(<Specialty> Subcombine <X>)`, the `Beta <Specialty> Research Kit`, and the specialty's field crafting tools (UO-06: `EAS-*` Electronics, `MAS-*` Materials, `BMAS-*` Biomedical; find the Power Systems prefix and record it). Take the research kit and the tools first, lowest `tech_comp` first; then the resources and subcombines by `tech_comp`, then `item_id`, until 20 rows. UO-06's lists 63804, 63805 and 63809 are candidates, not picks.
