## Why

Every Omega seed row that places something has to stand on the shipped navmesh, on the same component as its anchor. No coordinate may be guessed. This packet runs `nav_inspect` on each candidate and writes one table that every later packet copies verbatim. It changes no seed and no code. The coordinates are CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION, taken from the client's own mesh.

The owner's developer concept art ([omega-concept-art.md](../omega-concept-art.md), F27) shows the intended layout: a Common Area hub, an SGC (Human) wing to the west and a Free Jaffa wing to the east. It is RESTORED_SOURCE for layout intent only (D-UO15). This packet tags every chosen point with the art area it falls in and lists each disagreement with the art. It never moves a candidate because of the art: the mesh decides coordinates, and the orchestrator decides what to do about a disagreement.
