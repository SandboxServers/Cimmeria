## Why

The art draws an Infirmary in the hub, and the owner chose service NPCs wherever an engine service exists (D-UO19 c). A chain can heal and speak (F40): on an `interact_tag` chain the executor's entity is the clicking player, so `change_stat` refills that player's health and `npc_bark` speaks a seeded line to that player only. No code. The bark always fires, so a player at full health still gets feedback on the first press. Services pay nothing (D-UO37).
