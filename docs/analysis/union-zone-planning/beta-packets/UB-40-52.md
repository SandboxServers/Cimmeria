## UB-45b: cross-race guard, part 2 (login binds and transition consistency)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Role / size | `testing-validation-engineer`, about 40k (a design-heavy test, so not `packet-coder`) |
| Branch / worktree | `uzp-ub45b` / `uzp-ub45b`, stacked on `uzp-ub45a` |
| Commit subject | `test(beta-e1): UB-45b login binds match in-session binds on every walk transition` |

| Test files | `cross_race/login_binds.rs`, `cross_race/transitions.rs` (new); `cross_race/mod.rs` (two `mod` lines) |

**Why.** Binds are per player and not persisted (F14), so every in-session bind change needs a login twin, or a relog changes what the NPC says. F21 makes this sharper: a stale bind that no chain claims is auto-shown by the interact fallback. Each chain packet writes its own restore chains. UB-45b proves the whole set is consistent.

Rules: chain-packet-common's rules 1-10 and its lane block (filter `chain_replay_tests::beta_e1::cross_race`). Add no seed and no chain.

### `login_binds.rs`

- `login_binds_at_most_one_dialog_set_per_slot`: for every walk state, fire `login_ctx(BETA, Some(archetype))` and collect the `add_dialog_set(dsm, slot=t)` actions of all matched chains. No slot appears twice. A slot with two binds is the F21 hazard, because the fallback opens the first one.
- `no_beta_bind_outside_beta`: the same states with `login_ctx("Omega_Site", ..)` and `login_ctx("SGC_W1", ..)` bind nothing on slots 2300-2349. Copy the world names from `spaces.xml`; grep them, do not guess.

### `transitions.rs`

`transitions_keep_login_binds_consistent`. For each walk state `s`, each trigger key `e` (interact, choice, item use) and each archetype of the race:

1. Fire `e` at `s` and collect the actions in chain-id order.
2. Compute `s'` by applying: `accept_mission m` (m `active`, at its first step by `index`), `advance_step m x`, `complete_objective m o`, `complete_mission m` and `increment_counter`.
3. Follow-ups, at `s'`: for each `complete_mission m`, fire `completed_ctx(m, ..)` and append its actions. For each `accept_mission m`, fire the `mission_accepted` trigger if any Beta chain uses it.
4. `expected = login_binds(s)`, then apply every `add_dialog_set` and `remove_dialog_set` from steps 1 and 3 in order.
5. `actual = login_binds(s')`. Assert `expected == actual` as sets of `(dsm, slot)`.

Notes:
- Skip a transition when `s'` is not a walk state (for example a counter at 3); count the skips and print them.
- `KNOWN_DIVERGENCE: &[(&str, i64, i64)]` (state label, dsm, slot) starts **empty**. An entry needs a comment with the reason and the packet that owns it.
- The receivers are the expected exception to the "login equals session" rule: at an arrival step, login itself advances the step and binds. Model that by computing `login_binds(s)` from the full login resolution, receiver chains included.
- Revert proof: delete restore chain 23313 (481 S1762 login bind). The Carter click at S1761 (chain 23312, which moves to S1762) then expects `(2467, 2305)` while login at S1762 has none, and the test must fail. Restore it. Record both runs.

**Telemetry:** none (replay). UB-U3b and UB-U5b are the live relog checks.
