## Decision and scope

Run this as an **independent Free Jaffa route acceptance session after the Human route works**. Its playable target is a newly created Free Jaffa who starts on Dakara_E1, completes an honestly labelled level 1–2 bridge and the recovered Dakara crisis, makes the temporary SGC support trip, returns to Dakara, then reaches Omega and accepts the Free Jaffa `The SGU` assignment for Beta E1. The first Beta visit is the outbound acceptance boundary; [the Beta campaign](beta.md) owns Carter, Nine Peaks and antidote gameplay on world 23. The owner approved a short authored Dakara bridge on 2026-10-14. The Cellblock, Castle, Harset and Praxis Tollana continuations with FZ-99 must finish before the owning zones dispatch new implementation packets.

This is a route campaign that integrates **Dakara, SGC and Omega owners**. It does not claim those worlds exclusively or copy their chains. The coordinator can research and seal evidence independently, but implementation waits for the relevant Human zone contracts to exist on `main`. The older [Dakara_E1 rebuild](../dakara-e1-rebuild/README.md) is the initial world-61/62 ledger; reconcile its live status and inherit verified placements, identifiers and worknotes before replacing any work. An unfinished Dakara packet remains an inbound dependency, not a second copy of that packet.

## Decisions to settle before coding

1. **Level 1–2 bridge and 1570.** The recovered Dakara sequence is level 3–5 and its first Jaffa dialogue says “Welcome back” after an SGC attack. The current level-1 Dakara start is a project decision, and the owner approved a short authored introduction scouting divided Jaffa loyalties. The story seal must choose its exact reconnaissance beats, reward curve and truthful bridge into 1570's Dakara-local tasks or 1645. Foreshadow withdrawal-orders tensions without exposing Moh'katan's betrayal early; do not silently satisfy SGC objectives on a new character.
2. **GDK1 combat.** DK-20/21/32 remain subject to the Dakara encounter and level-band design gate. Plan small server-authoritative fights, but freeze counts, spaces, pacing and rewards only after the client placement walk and owner choice.
3. **Rewards and level curve.** The seeded Dakara missions carry no reliable original reward package. Make XP, gear, item and combat sustain explicit project policy, test reward once and ensure level 5 before Omega/Beta. Do not infer rewards from mission level labels.
4. **World transitions.** Confirm SGC gate behavior and world-80 Command Center mapping in the target client build. Until then, world IDs and task text are evidence; coordinates are not sealed.
5. **Early Omega address.** DK-01 already grants the Omega address so a level-1 Jaffa can leave a dead-end starter. Preserve that escape route while restoring the story; gate Bra'tac's 1655 completion and mission 1500 on actual quest state, so visiting Omega early cannot skip Dakara or SGC.

## Definition of done

A fresh Free Jaffa can finish the authored opening, the selected recovered Dakara branch, both SGC visits and Bra'tac's Omega handoff, then reach Beta E1 with 1500 active for Carter. No GM command, manual database edit, Human mission credit or borrowed Asgard state is involved. Chain-replay and live-DB tests cover grants, step order, reward once, abandon/retry, relog and travel; two-player and wrong-faction tests guard Dakara and Omega phasing. A client walkthrough after explicit lab approval proves the map, dialog, beam and DHD path. The ledger distinguishes every source-backed, reconstructed and authored beat, and marks any unobserved client behavior `UATPending` rather than `Done`.
