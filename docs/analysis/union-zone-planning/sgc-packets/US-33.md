## Goal

Two parts.

**A. 1655 for a Free Jaffa (archetype 7).** After 1654, Woolsey's press shows 5855 ("…your services as liaison to the Jaffa on Dakara are no longer needed.") and accepts 1655 on the press: 5855 has Accept on screens 0-2 only and fails linter R1, so it is **never keyed** (F6). Hammond's press on 4943 shows 5875 ("…I have a message for you to deliver to Bra'tac… Speak to Sgt. Harriman…"); closing it advances to 4944 and lights Harriman. The Free Jaffa gets **no** letter item (D-US11 default, H-O6). From 4944 the shared Harriman beat of US-32 (3160-3165) dials Omega Site for both races.

**B. `disjoint.rs`**, a whole-range suite that proves the guards the campaign relies on: world guards, archetype guards, the F13 rule, no Human tutorial credit for a Free Jaffa, and two distinguishable letter recipients for Omega.

Everything is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION except the dialog text (RESTORED_SOURCE).
