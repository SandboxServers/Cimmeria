## Goal

**1651** starts on Dakara (the Dakara campaign owns its first step, 4930). Its SGC half:

| Step | Text | Required objectives |
|---|---|---|
| 4928 | Speak to Gen. Hammond. | 5955 |
| 4931 | Meet Col. Carter in the Gate Room to receive the naquadah generators. | **6037** ("Speak to Sgt. Harriman in the Control Room to dial the Stargate to Dakara.") and **5956** |
| 4929 | Gate to Dakara with the naquadah generators. | 5957 |
| 5171 | Report to Master Bra'tac at the Command Tent. | 5984 (Dakara side) |

Flow, for **both races** (1651 has no archetype gate; step state is the gate):

1. The player arrives from Dakara (crossing C2, owned by DK-30) with step 4928 active. Hammond wears "!".
2. Hammond's press shows 5844 (Hammond and Carter plan the naquadah overload). Closing it advances to 4931, moves Carter to the Gate Room (P08) and puts "!" on her.
3. Carter's press shows 5845. Its "Receive Item" button grants the generator (item 6784) and completes O5956. O6037 is still open, so the step holds (F13). Carter's "!" moves to Harriman.
4. Harriman's press dials out (crossing **C2b**, F4 pattern): advance to 4929 at once (force-completes O6037), the dial sequences, then 5171 and the cross-world teleport to Dakara_E1 three seconds later.
5. Restore chains re-light the right NPC on a relog; a recovery press finishes an interrupted dial.

Everything is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION except the dialog text (RESTORED_SOURCE, already seeded).

**The F13 trap, stated for this packet.** `complete_objective` completes the whole mission when it closes the last open required objective of the current step. On 4931 that is safe for O5956 only because O6037 is still open. **Never** `complete_objective 1651 '6037'` and never `complete_objective` on 4928 or 4929: each would complete 1651 at the SGC and break the Dakara half. Every step change in this packet is an `advance_step`.
