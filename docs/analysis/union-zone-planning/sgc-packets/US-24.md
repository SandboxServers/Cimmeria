## Goal

**1570** has three steps:

| Step | Text | Required objective |
|---|---|---|
| 4641 | Speak to Sgt. Harriman to dial the Stargate to Dakara. | 5382 |
| 4642 | Enter the Stargate to travel to Dakara. | 5383 |
| 4906 | Speak to Bra'tac at his command tent. | (Dakara side; the Dakara campaign owns it) |

When a Human has completed both 1568 and 1569, Hammond and Teal'c each wear "?" (H3). Hammond's 5392 or Teal'c's 5393 offers 1570; either choice accepts it, clears both "?", puts "!" on Harriman and shows the follow-up line (5395 after Hammond, 5396 after Teal'c). Harriman's press dials out (crossing **C1**, F4 pattern): advance to 4642 at once, the dial sequences, then 4906 and the cross-world teleport to Dakara_E1 three seconds later. Restore and recovery chains cover a relog or a disconnect inside the three-second window.

Everything is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION except the dialog text (RESTORED_SOURCE, already seeded).

  1. `advance_step` to the intermediate step, delay 0
  2. `play_sequence 10047`, delay 0
  3. `play_sequence 10060`, delay 2500
  4. `advance_step` to the arrival step, delay 3000
