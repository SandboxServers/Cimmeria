## Goal

After a Human finishes 1562, Carter shows "?". Her 5370 offers 1563; accepting it marks her "!". Pressing her again shows 5371 (her notes) and moves to step 4629, which lights her terminal. Pressing the terminal shows 5372 and completes objective 5369, so 1563 completes. Every marker comes back on relog. D-US7 default: a plain interact, no hack minigame. Every offer and position here is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION; the steps, objectives and dialogs are RESTORED_SOURCE.
