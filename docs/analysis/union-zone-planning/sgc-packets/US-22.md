| Step | Text | Required objective |

| 4637 | Make your way to Gate Control on sub-level 27. | 5378 (5831 is hidden optional; leave it) |
| 4638 | Use the control terminal to deactivate the Stargate. | 5379 |
| 4639 | Eliminate the remaining Jaffa in the Gate Room. | 5380 |

The server offers 1568 to a Human when 1564 completes (or on load after it). Entering Gate Control advances to 4638, plays the monologue 5382 and lights the terminal `SGC_W1_GateTerminal1`.
