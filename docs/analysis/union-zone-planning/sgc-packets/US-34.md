## Goal

SGC_W1 is instanced **one instance per player** (`entities/spaces.xml:39` `Instanced="true"`; `space_manager/lifecycle.rs` destroys the space when its last player leaves). Every return rebuilds the instance from the spawnlist, so the story Jaffa a player already killed stand up again. Players come back through C2b (1651, from Dakara), C3 (1654) and a plain relog.

On `player_loaded` this packet despawns, for that player's instance only, the tagged story Jaffa whose missions are spent. It builds the D-US5 default:

- no Gate Room Jaffa once 1568 is done;
- no story Jaffa at all for a Free Jaffa;
- a route pair stays only while a mission still needs it.

Carter in the Gate Room during 4931 is US-30's chain 3139 and is not re-done here. Rows 71 and 77 (the 1562 lab attackers, untagged) are not touched. Everything here is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION.
