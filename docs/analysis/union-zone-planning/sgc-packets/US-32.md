## Goal

**1655** has five steps; the SGC owns the first three and the C4 departure:

| Step | Text | Required objectives | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4943 | Find out from Gen. Hammond if the SGC is really shutting down. | 5963 | SGC (US-32 Human, US-33 Free Jaffa) |
| 4944 | Dial the gate to the classified address Hammond gave you. | 5983 ("Speak to Sgt. Harriman in the control room to dial the Stargate.") | SGC, **both races** (this packet) |
| 4941 | Travel through the Stargate to Omega Site. | 6055 | the C4 window |
| 4940 | Beam to the Command Center aboard the Asgard Capitol Ship. | 5647, 5644 (5645, 5646 optional) | Omega |
| 4942 | Deliver Hammond's letter. | 6056 (Daniel), 6058 (Bra'tac) | Omega |

Flow:

1. After 1654, Woolsey (spawned by US-31 at P02) wears "?". A Human's press shows 5854 (Woolsey announces the closure); its Accept accepts 1655, clears the "?" and puts "!" on Hammond.
2. Hammond's press shows 5856 to a Human. Closing it grants the letter (item 6786, "Letter to Daniel Jackson", D-US11 default), advances to 4944 and moves the "!" to Harriman.
3. On 4944 (either race; US-33 brings the Free Jaffa here), Harriman's press shows 5857 ("He's already informed me that I should send you along to the classified location."). Closing it dials out (crossing **C4**, F4 pattern): advance to 4941 at once, the dial sequences, then 4940 and the cross-world teleport to Omega_Site three seconds later.
4. Restore and recovery chains cover a relog or a disconnect inside the window.

Every step change here is an `advance_step`. **Never `complete_objective` on 1655 at the SGC**: 4942 is the last step, and completing the only required objective of 4943, 4944 or 4941 completes the whole mission (F13).

Everything is CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION except the dialog text (RESTORED_SOURCE).
