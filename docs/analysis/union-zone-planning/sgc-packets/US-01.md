## Goal

Every coordinate an SGC packet writes into a seed comes from this packet's `placement.md`. The index lists proposals (§ Placement points). Probe each one on the SGC_W1 navmesh, move it if it misses, and record the sealed value with its evidence. SGC_W1 is instanced and `validate_gate_arrival` skips instanced worlds, so nothing at runtime catches a bad arrival point: this packet is the only check.

All points are CIMMERIA_RECONSTRUCTION.

## Steps

### 1. Probe every proposal

Write `probes/sgc-w1-points.txt` with P01, P02, P04-P16 (P03 is dropped), the six crate points P10a-f, and three references: the gate 27 row, Hammond's row 52, and the arrival `ARRIVAL` (172.79, -10.9, 12.0). Run the lane command once with `--gaps`. Quote the output in the report.

For each point record: in tolerance or not, the poly's height, the component id.

### 2. Rules a sealed point must meet

| Rule | Points |
|---|---|
| On a poly within 1.0 m horizontally and 1.0 m vertically | all |
| In the same navmesh component as the arrival P13 (the player can walk to it) | P02, P04-P09, P10a-f, P13-P16 |
| An interact target is within 4 m of a reachable standing point (server range 5.0 m), with open floor behind the player | P01 (Harriman), P04, P06, P10a-f, and the gate terminal (US-22) |
| An NPC point does not overlap another NPC or a static mesh (at least 1.0 m apart) | P01, P02, P05, P08, P11, P12, P14, P16 |
| The player's teleport point (P13, P15) sits on the floor, not inside furniture, with at least 1.5 m clear around it | P13, P15 |
| A region centre (P07 r 8 h 6, P09 r 10 h 6) covers its room and does not reach a neighbouring corridor the player crosses on another step | P07, P09 |
| The 1566 Jaffa pair (P11, P12) spawns out of sight of the player's 1565 fight and on the way to the Failsafe room, and its aggro radius does not reach the Failsafe room (npc-ai-spawn-advisor) | P11, P12 |

A point that fails is moved to the nearest point that passes; record old, new and why. P13 must stay in front of gate 27 on the Gate Room floor (y about -10.9); never on the gate row.

### 3. NPC review (npc-ai-spawn-advisor, read-only)

For P01 (Harriman seated, heading 3.1416), P02 (Woolsey beside Hammond), P05 and P08 (Carter), P11 and P12 (template 34 Jaffa), P14 (Hammond home), P16 (Carter after 1568): check heading, idle versus patrol, aggro and leash radius for the hostile pair, and that a friendly NPC point sits outside the 1562 attackers' aggro radius (rows 71/77). Write the findings into the table's Notes column.
