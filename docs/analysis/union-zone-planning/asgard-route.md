## Decision and scope

Run this as an **independent Asgard route acceptance session after the Human route works**. The owner chose a **post-Pertho SGU prologue** using new content in existing Omega map geometry. A fresh Asgard enters after Pertho's evacuation, learns what happened, secures rescued consciousness databanks, receives a **remote** Council ruling, joins the public Omega SGU hub and reaches the first Beta E1 assignment. The [post-Pertho brief](asgard-post-pertho.md) governs its story shape. This plan checks zone-owned execution against the source-backed boundary through accepted Beta arrival; it does not restore the missing Pertho or physical High Council maps.

Implementation remains behind the completed Cellblock, Castle, Harset and Praxis Tollana continuations and their FZ-99 close-out. The Union Human route is first. No Asgard runtime packet starts merely because this planning PR merges. The existing [class-start v6](../class-start-v6/README.md) labels Asgard char-def 9's SGC_W1 tutorial and universal kit a `NON_CANONICAL_BLOCKED_LEGACY` holding state. This campaign removes that holding state for the Asgard path in one reviewed transition; it must not disrupt the Human SGC tutorial or Free Jaffa Dakara start.

## Gates that cannot be guessed

1. **Private space and presentation.** `Omega_Site` world 18 and `Omega_Site_CmdCenter` world 80 have production maps; the other declared Omega submaps 81/82 lack audited production packages. A client-loaded alias with collision, navmesh, minimap, travel and logout safety must be proved before placement. If the alias fails, select another existing shipped space only through a documented visual/story fit and client proof. Do not fall back to an unisolated public hub.
2. **Remote Council actors.** Otin is named by mission/dialog text, but the Council master does not establish a safe speaker/template ID for him. The story seal may use verified speakers or a clearly new representative. Do not assign speaker 2509 to Otin by guesswork.
3. **Stock mission compatibility.** Original 1642 expects a physical Council departure; do not run that travel step in the new entry. 1522's Otin/High Council objective and item 3399's physical issue point need a per-step proof. A new mission is preferable to showing an impossible place or awarding a quest before its stated authority exists.
4. **New client content and pacing.** The authored mission catalog path must present titles, objectives and dialogue in the real client before scaling the prologue. Level/reward pacing targets Beta's level-6 481 without fabricating original Pertho rewards or using a GM level adjustment. The story decision should change a bounded state or follow-up line, never block the common hub.

## Definition of done

A newly created Asgard receives the post-Pertho briefing, completes a coherent private recovery and remote hearing, joins the public SGU at Omega and reaches Beta E1 with a truthful, Asgard-only 481 assignment. The player never sees Human SGC or Free Jaffa Dakara starter state, unearned Pertho completion, an impossible physical Council objective, or tutorial actors in public Omega. Chain-replay and live-DB guards cover once-only grants, choices, relog, reward, travel and wrong-faction refusals; a two-player test proves private phase isolation. After explicit lab approval, client UAT covers world loading, map presentation, navigation, dialogue, the combat drone, item 3399, mission catalog and Beta travel. Any item the client run cannot verify stays `UATPending`.
