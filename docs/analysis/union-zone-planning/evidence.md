## Source order

The primary planning input is Lomiada's [SGW Claude Server Handoff Pack v1.2](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/README.md), especially its [source policy](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/docs/SOURCE_POLICY.md), [world index](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/docs/WORLD_CONTENT_INDEX.md), and rendered [Human prologue](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_SGU_Human_Full_Starter_Prologue_to_Omega_v2.md), [Free Jaffa prologue](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Free_Jaffa_Full_Starter_Prologue_to_Omega_v2.md), [Pertho](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Pertho_Asgard_Starter_Dev_Master_v1.md), [High Council](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Asgard_High_Council_Dev_Master_v1.md), [Omega](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Omega_Site_Dev_Master_v1.md), and [Beta](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Beta_Site_Complete_Dev_Master_v1.md) workbooks. These are an external research handoff, not an original server export. The pack itself warns that its machine-readable data partly derives from this repository's seed. A match with the seed therefore does not independently prove 2009 behavior.

The on-disk `SGW_FINAL_DEVELOPER_HANDOFF.zip` supplements the pack. Its exact world dossiers have 13 SGC_W1, 12 Omega_Site, 76 Beta_Site_Evo_1 and 51 Tollana **mission-to-world moniker associations**. Those are historical associations, not playable chain counts. Its master handoff names `SGW_FINAL_RE.sqlite` and `SGW_ALL_FINDINGS_ARCHIVE.sqlite`; neither was supplied, as recorded by the [first four-zone evidence inventory](../four-zone-planning/downloads-evidence.md). No missing join may be invented. The archive's `WORLDS/RECOVERED/` directory has dossiers for SGC, SGC_W1, Dakara_E1 and its Story Room, Omega_Site, Omega_Site_CmdCenter, Beta_Site_Evo_1 and Tollana. The [Dakara_E1 audit](../dakara-e1-rebuild/audit.md) found its archive mission list incomplete and its spawn/encounter files empty. The archive has no Beta_Site_E2 or SGC_W2 dossier. The map package and build of each candidate must be recorded before a placement is sealed.

For runtime truth, compare those claims with `db/resources/Missions/Seed/`, `db/resources/Content/Seed/`, `db/resources/Worlds/Seed/`, [mission chains](../../content/mission-chains.md), the [class-start v6 ledger](../class-start-v6/README.md), [Dakara rebuild ledger](../dakara-e1-rebuild/README.md), and [first four-zone plan](../four-zone-planning/README.md). The [external data analysis](../../content/external-data-analysis.md) describes the supplied Progression and Timeline workbooks, but its coarse route cannot supply an exact mission grant edge.

**Convergence has two milestones.** All three paths first share a playable SGU hub at **Omega Site**. Their first common field map is **Beta Site E1**. Reaching Omega does not imply that a character has completed its faction-specific induction, and reaching Beta does not make the three mission families interchangeable. The [Omega master](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Omega_Site_Dev_Master_v1.md) and [Beta master](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Beta_Site_Complete_Dev_Master_v1.md) are the source checks for those boundaries.
