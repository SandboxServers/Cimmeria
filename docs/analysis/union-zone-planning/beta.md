## Playable outcome

Each SGU starting race arrives from its own Omega handoff and begins the first Beta Site E1 field story. Human reports to Carter and joins closure/research work; Free Jaffa reaches the Nine Peaks liaison and antidote thread; Asgard reports to Carter/Mimir and begins the source-backed observation and disease investigation. All three can share the world while the server keeps their mission offers, interactions, items and phase-sensitive actors separate.

The [Beta master](../sgw-handoff-pack-v1.2/pack/references/world_content/SGW_Beta_Site_Complete_Dev_Master_v1.md) identifies world 23/Gate 6 for the first SGU visit, with a main production UMAP, MapData, 630 streaming cells, minimap and gate Kismet. It separates an uncertain Closed-Beta return and late `Beta_Site_E2` world 60 joint SGU/Praxis crisis; those are **later visits**, not the first convergence. World 60 lacks a named production UMAP/MapData in the audited handoff. Visit 2 also contains placeholder dialogue and overlapping revisions. This campaign's first acceptance boundary is E1; any later-visit work needs its own evidence and map seal.
